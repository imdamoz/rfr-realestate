( 03 ) COMMON QUESTIONS ABOUT AL HAMRA VILLAGE

Can foreigners buy property in Al Hamra Village?

Yes. Al Hamra Village is one of Ras Al Khaimah’s designated freehold areas, so foreign buyers can own property there. RAK Municipality lists the freehold areas as Al Hamra Village, Mina Al Arab, Al Marjan Island, RAK Central, Julphar Towers, Al Maareedh (plots) and the upcoming Beach District.

How much does property cost in Al Hamra Village?

Apartments start from about AED 500K, and townhouses and villas sit at about AED 1.5–5M. These ranges are indicative, based on RFR listings and public launch data.

How much is rent in Al Hamra Village?

A one-bedroom rents for about AED 45–65K a year and a three-bedroom villa for about AED 110–160K a year. Figures are indicative; exact rents for any unit are available on request.

What rental yield can I expect in Al Hamra Village?

About 7–8.5% gross, as an indicative range. It is the closest established community to Al Marjan Island, so it’s where the resort staff arriving in 2027 will rent.

Who lives in Al Hamra Village and what is nearby?

Families, golfers, Al Hamra and Wynn workforce, and investors who want tenants in place from day one. Al Hamra Golf Club, Al Hamra Marina & Yacht Club, Al Hamra Mall, the Waldorf Astoria and Falcon Island are on the doorstep, and Dubai (DXB) is about 50 minutes away.
