Al Marjan Island apartments & property

Four coral-shaped islands and the new resorts.

Al Marjan Island apartments start from about AED 920K for a studio, and one- and two-bedroom branded residences sit at about AED 1.7–3.5M. A one-bedroom rents for about AED 65–90K a year and a two-bedroom for about AED 75–130K. The island is a designated freehold area, so foreign buyers can own here.

A group of man-made islands off the Al Hamra coast. Most of RAK’s branded residences are being built here, including Address, Nobu, JW Marriott, Nikki Beach and W, and the Wynn resort is due to open in 2027.

( 03 ) COMMON QUESTIONS ABOUT AL MARJAN ISLAND

Can foreigners buy property on Al Marjan Island?

Yes. Al Marjan Island is one of Ras Al Khaimah’s designated freehold areas, so foreign buyers can own property there. RAK Municipality lists the freehold areas as Al Hamra Village, Mina Al Arab, Al Marjan Island, RAK Central, Julphar Towers, Al Maareedh (plots) and the upcoming Beach District.

How much does an apartment cost on Al Marjan Island?

Studios start from about AED 920K, and one- and two-bedroom branded residences sit at about AED 1.7–3.5M. These ranges are indicative, based on RFR listings and public launch data.

How much is rent on Al Marjan Island?

A one-bedroom rents for about AED 65–90K a year and a two-bedroom for about AED 75–130K a year. Figures are indicative; exact rents for any unit are available on request.

What rental yield can I expect on Al Marjan Island?

About 6–8% gross, as an indicative range. Ask RFR for the figures on a specific unit.

Who lives on Al Marjan Island and what is nearby?

Overseas investors, holiday-let owners, resort professionals and second-home buyers from Dubai. Wynn Al Marjan Island is due in 2027, there are public and resort beaches, Al Hamra Mall and Al Hamra Marina are about 12 minutes away, RAK Airport about 35 minutes and Dubai (DXB) about 55 minutes.
