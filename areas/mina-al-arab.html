( 03 ) COMMON QUESTIONS ABOUT MINA AL ARAB

Can foreigners buy property in Mina Al Arab?

Yes. Mina Al Arab is one of Ras Al Khaimah’s designated freehold areas, so foreign buyers can own property there. RAK Municipality lists the freehold areas as Al Hamra Village, Mina Al Arab, Al Marjan Island, RAK Central, Julphar Towers, Al Maareedh (plots) and the upcoming Beach District.

How much does property cost in Mina Al Arab?

Apartments start from about AED 800K, and villas sit at about AED 2.5–6M. These ranges are indicative, based on RFR listings and public launch data.

How much is rent in Mina Al Arab?

A three-bedroom villa rents for about AED 85–140K a year and a two-bedroom apartment for about AED 60–95K a year. Figures are indicative; exact rents for any unit are available on request.

What rental yield can I expect in Mina Al Arab?

About 5.5–7% gross, as an indicative range. Ask RFR for the figures on a specific unit.

Who lives in Mina Al Arab and what is nearby?

Families relocating to RAK, long-term residents, and buyers who want water without the resort crowds. The Anantara and InterContinental resorts, the mangrove beach and boardwalks are on the doorstep, RAK Academy is about 10 minutes away, RAK city centre about 15 minutes and RAK Airport about 30 minutes.
