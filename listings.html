    ( FAQ )

    Common questions.

    What property is for sale in Ras Al Khaimah with RFR?

    Ready homes and off-plan units on Al Marjan Island, Mina Al Arab, Al Hamra Village and Downtown RAK, updated as our book changes.

    Can foreigners buy property in Ras Al Khaimah?

    Yes, in the designated freehold areas. Al Marjan Island, Mina Al Arab and Al Hamra Village are all freehold, so foreign buyers can own outright there. In Downtown RAK, freehold is limited to designated projects such as Julphar Towers and RAK Central.

    Which areas of Ras Al Khaimah does RFR cover?

    Al Marjan Island, Mina Al Arab, Al Hamra Village and Downtown RAK. We don’t work outside Ras Al Khaimah.

    How do off-plan payment plans work in Ras Al Khaimah?

    Buyers pay in instalments as the building is constructed, typically on a schedule between 50/50 and 80/20, with the balance due on or near handover.

    Do I need to be in the UAE to buy property in Ras Al Khaimah?

    No. Many of our buyers live overseas. We run video viewings and can handle the paperwork and management once you own it.
