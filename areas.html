Al Marjan Island

Four coral-shaped islands and the new resorts.

A group of man-made islands off the Al Hamra coast. Most of RAK’s branded residences are being built here, including Address, Nobu, JW Marriott, Nikki Beach and W, and the Wynn resort is due to open in 2027.

To buy
From ~AED 920K (studio) · AED 1.7–3.5M for 1–2 bed branded
To rent
1-bed ~AED 65–90K / yr · 2-bed ~AED 75–130K / yr
Gross yield
6–8% gross (indicative)

Who lives here

Overseas investors, holiday-let owners, resort professionals and second-home buyers from Dubai.

Nearby

  • Wynn Al Marjan Island (2027)
  • Public and resort beaches
  • Al Hamra Mall12 min
  • Al Hamra Marina12 min
  • RAK Airport35 min
  • Dubai (DXB)55 min

Supply is finite by design: the islands are fully master-planned, so the current launch wave is most of what will ever exist here.

( 02 ) Area guide

Mina Al Arab

Lagoons, mangroves and the quiet side of the coast.

A low-rise waterfront community built around lagoons and protected mangroves, anchored by the Anantara and InterContinental resorts. Villas, townhouses and lagoon-front apartments in an established, family-led neighbourhood.

To buy
Apartments from ~AED 800K · Villas AED 2.5–6M
To rent
3-bed villa ~AED 85–140K / yr · 2-bed apt ~AED 60–95K / yr
Gross yield
5.5–7% gross (indicative)

Who lives here

Families relocating to RAK, long-term residents, and buyers who want water without the resort crowds.

Nearby

  • Anantara Mina Al Arab
  • InterContinental Mina Al Arab
  • Mangrove beach and boardwalks
  • RAK Academy10 min
  • RAK city centre15 min
  • RAK Airport30 min

The Ritz-Carlton Residences and Porto Playa are the next phase: the first branded product on this side of the coast.

( 03 ) Area guide

Al Hamra Village

Golf, marina and the deepest rental market in RAK.

The Emirate’s original master community: an 18-hole championship golf course, a marina and yacht club, the Waldorf Astoria, a mall and a decade of tenants. Falcon Island, its private villa enclave, sits within it.

To buy
Apartments from ~AED 500K · Townhouses and villas AED 1.5–5M
To rent
1-bed ~AED 45–65K / yr · 3-bed villa ~AED 110–160K / yr
Gross yield
7–8.5% gross (indicative)

Who lives here

Families, golfers, Al Hamra and Wynn workforce, and investors who want tenants in place from day one.

Nearby

  • Al Hamra Golf Club
  • Al Hamra Marina & Yacht Club
  • Al Hamra Mall
  • Waldorf Astoria RAK
  • Falcon Island
  • Dubai (DXB)50 min

Closest established community to Al Marjan Island, so it’s where the resort staff arriving in 2027 will rent.

Downtown RAK

The working city: corniche, hospitals, offices.

Al Nakheel, Julphar and the corniche, where the Emirate actually works. Towers and mid-rise apartments with the highest yields and the shortest commutes to RAKEZ, the hospitals and government.

To buy
Apartments from ~AED 400K
To rent
2-bed ~AED 50–95K / yr
Gross yield
7–9% gross (indicative)

Who lives here

Professionals, RAKEZ business owners, medical and education staff.

Nearby

  • RAK Hospital
  • Corniche Al Qawasim
  • Manar Mall
  • Julphar Towers
  • RAKEZ10 min
  • RAK Airport20 min

Less glamour, more cash flow. Where we send clients who care about net yield over sea view.
