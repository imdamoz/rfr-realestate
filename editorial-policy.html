Editorial policy

RFR REAL ESTATE & ADVISORY FZ-LLC · UPDATED 15 NOVEMBER 2026

This page explains who publishes www.rfrrealestate.com, what our guides are, how we date our sources and what we do not publish.

( 01 ) PUBLISHER

Who publishes this site

This website is published by RFR Real Estate & Advisory FZ-LLC (“RFR Real Estate”), a licensed real estate brokerage and advisory firm in Ras Al Khaimah, United Arab Emirates. We work only in Ras Al Khaimah: Al Marjan Island, Mina Al Arab, Al Hamra Village and Downtown RAK.

We are not RFR Holding, the New York real estate company that can also appear in searches for “RFR”. More about us is on the About page.

( 02 ) THE GUIDES

What our guides are

Our guides are informational pages about property in Ras Al Khaimah: buying, the areas we cover, off-plan launches and the UAE Golden Visa property route. They are written by the RFR advisory team and carry the date they were published or last updated.

They are general information, not legal, financial, tax or immigration advice. RFR is a brokerage that sells, leases and manages property in Ras Al Khaimah, so we have a commercial interest in the market we write about. Price, rent and yield ranges that we mark as indicative are based on RFR listings and public launch data.

( 03 ) SOURCES

How we source and date facts

Where a guide relies on an official or published source, such as Ras Al Khaimah Municipality, ICP, the u.ae portal, RAKEZ, or published market reports and press coverage, the guide names the source and gives the date we checked it. Rules, fees and figures change, so that date tells you how current our check was. Confirm anything you plan to rely on with the relevant authority before you act.

( 04 ) WHEN SOURCES DIFFER

When official sources disagree

If official sources say different things, we set out what each one says rather than choosing between them. For example, official pages we checked on 15 November 2026 do not agree on how long the property Golden Visa lasts: ICP and RAKEZ describe 10 years, while the u.ae portal’s eligibility table lists 5 years for real estate investments. We do not say which applies to you. Our Golden Visa guide shows each source and its date; confirm the term in writing with ICP or the Ras Al Khaimah channel you apply through.

( 05 ) WHAT WE DO NOT PUBLISH

What we do not publish

  • Invented reviews or testimonials, star ratings or review scores. We do not add rating or review markup to our pages.
  • Our trade licence number. RFR Real Estate & Advisory FZ-LLC is licensed in Ras Al Khaimah, and the licence number is available on request.
  • A street address. We do not publish one on this site; contact is by phone, WhatsApp or email.
