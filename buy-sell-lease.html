( Buying guide )

How buying works in Ras Al Khaimah.

Freehold, tax-free and simpler than most buyers expect. Here is the route from first call to keys.

  1. 01Brief and shortlist

    Budget, purpose and timing. We shortlist ready homes and launches worth your time, and say which to skip.

  2. 02Viewings and checks

    In person, or on video from abroad. We check the developer, the service charge and the title before you commit.

  3. 03Offer and paperwork

    We negotiate the price or the payment plan, then the sale agreement and the developer’s no-objection certificate.

  4. 04Transfer and keys

    Registration with the RAK Land Department, then handover. The same advisor stays with you afterwards.

( Selling guide )

How selling works with us.

Start with the number. A free review tells you what your home would sell for and what it would rent for, so you decide with the facts.

  1. 01Free review

    Sale value, achievable rent and our advice: sell, hold or re-let. Back within 48 hours.

  2. 02Price and prepare

    An asking price based on recent sales, not hope, plus photos and a listing that does the home justice.

  3. 03Viewings and offers

    We show it, qualify every buyer and negotiate on your side of the table.

  4. 04Transfer

    The paperwork, the no-objection certificate and the transfer, through to handing over the keys.

( Leasing guide )

Renting or letting, done properly.

Two sides of the same contract. Here is how each one works with us.

If you’re renting

  1. 01
    Budget and area

    Tell us the rent, the size and where. We shortlist only homes that are actually available.

  2. 02
    Viewings this week

    In person or on video. Furnished island units move fast.

  3. 03
    Contract and cheques

    Most landlords here accept up to 4 cheques a year.

  4. 04
    Keys

    Move-in, utilities and one contact for anything after.

If you’re letting

  1. 01
    Rent estimate

    What it will let for, furnished or not.

  2. 02
    The right tenant

  3. 03
    Contract
