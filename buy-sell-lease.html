( Buyer’s guide )

Buying property in Ras Al Khaimah as a foreigner.

Who can buy, where, what it costs and the steps to the title deed. Freehold areas and fees are as published by Ras Al Khaimah Municipality (Lands & Properties Sector), checked on 14 November 2026.

Can foreigners buy property in Ras Al Khaimah?

Yes, in the Emirate’s designated freehold areas. Ras Al Khaimah Municipality lists them as Al Hamra Village, Mina Al Arab, Al Marjan Island, RAK Central, Julphar Towers, Al Maareedh (plots) and the upcoming Beach District. UAE and GCC nationals can buy anywhere in the Emirate.

Freehold means full ownership in your own name, recorded on a title deed. Before you pay a deposit, confirm that the exact plot or building sits inside a freehold area; in Downtown RAK, freehold is limited to designated projects such as Julphar Towers and RAK Central.

Do you need to be in the UAE to buy?

Not necessarily. We run video viewings for overseas buyers, and a power of attorney can be used if someone acts for you at the transfer.

Is there tax on property in the UAE?

There is no income tax or capital gains tax on property in the UAE. The registration fees below still apply when you buy.

What does it cost to buy?

The main government cost is the registration fee: 2% of the property’s market value, paid by the buyer. The seller pays a further 2%. Two small issuance fees and, if you borrow, a mortgage registration fee are added on top.

ItemAmountNotes
Registration fee (buyer)2% of market valuePaid to RAK Municipality
Registration fee (seller)2% of market valuePaid by the seller
Plan issuanceAED 200
Title deed issuanceAED 200
Mortgage registration0.1% of the loanOnly where there is a mortgage
Agency commissionAgreed with the agentNot set by the authorities. Ours is 2% + VAT, in writing before we start.
Developer chargesSet by the developerNot set by the authorities

As a worked example, on an AED 1,000,000 apartment bought without a mortgage the buyer’s government fees come to AED 20,400: AED 20,000 for registration and AED 400 for the plan and title deed.

How to buy, step by step

  1. Set your budget and goal. Decide whether the property is to live in, to rent out or for a Golden Visa, and include the 2% registration fee in the budget.
  2. Shortlist an area. Match the goal to the area: yield in Downtown RAK or Al Hamra Village, space in Mina Al Arab, resort-led off-plan on Al Marjan Island. Compare the four areas.
  3. View the property. In person, or on video if you are abroad.
  4. Reserve and sign. Agree the price and sign a memorandum of understanding (MOU) for a resale, or the developer’s sale and purchase agreement (SPA) for off-plan.
  5. Get the developer NOC. In freehold areas the developer issues a no-objection certificate before the transfer.
  6. Transfer and register. Complete the transfer at RAK Municipality, pay the registration fee and receive the title deed.
  7. Take handover, or let it out. Move in, or let our property management team find and manage tenants.

Documents for a resale purchase

  • Emirates ID or passport
  • A no-objection certificate (NOC) from the developer, for freehold areas
  • The seller’s original title deed
  • A power of attorney, if an agent acts for either party

For an off-plan unit you sign the developer’s SPA and pay against its schedule.

Does buying give you a Golden Visa?

It can. Property worth AED 2M or more can qualify you for a UAE Golden Visa, subject to the federal ICP rules at the time you apply. Official pages differ on the term: ICP and RAKEZ describe 10 years, while the u.ae portal lists 5 years for real estate investments. RAKEZ states that the applicant must be inside the UAE when submitting the request.

If the visa is the goal, tell us at the start, because it shapes the budget and the choice of unit. RFR does not issue visas or give immigration advice.

General information, not legal advice. Rules and fees can change; confirm them with the authority, or ask us, before you commit.

( FAQ )

Common questions.

Can foreigners buy property in Ras Al Khaimah?

Yes, in the designated freehold areas. Ras Al Khaimah Municipality lists them as Al Hamra Village, Mina Al Arab, Al Marjan Island, RAK Central, Julphar Towers, Al Maareedh (plots) and the upcoming Beach District. UAE and GCC nationals can buy anywhere in the Emirate.

Does buying property in Ras Al Khaimah give me a Golden Visa?

It can. Property worth AED 2M or more can qualify you for a UAE Golden Visa, subject to the federal ICP rules at the time you apply. Official pages differ on whether the property route gives 5 or 10 years, so confirm the term with ICP or your RAK channel. RFR does not issue visas or give immigration advice.

What government fees do I pay when buying in Ras Al Khaimah?

The buyer pays a registration fee of 2% of the market value to RAK Municipality, plus AED 200 for plan issuance and AED 200 for the title deed. If you use a mortgage, its registration costs 0.1% of the loan. Agency commission and developer charges are separate.

Do I need to be in the UAE to buy?

Not necessarily. We run viewings over video for buyers abroad, a power of attorney can be used if someone acts for you at the transfer, and we can manage the property once you own it.

Is there tax on property in the UAE?

There is no income tax or capital gains tax on property in the UAE. The registration fees still apply when you buy.

What’s the difference between buying off-plan and buying a ready home?

Off-plan means paying in instalments for a home that isn’t finished yet, usually at a lower entry price. A ready home costs more upfront but you can view and move into it straight away.
