What the Wynn opening means for Al Marjan, and what to do about it.

By the RFR Advisory team · 4 min read

( 01 ) The headline

One resort opening is repricing a coastline.

Wynn Al Marjan Island is scheduled to open in 2027, and almost everything else on the island is being priced against that date. Our current book carries 9 launches on Al Marjan alone, with developer entry prices from AED 920,000 to AED 4.06M.

The names tell you who believes the story: Emaar, Aldar, Ellington, Marriott (JW and W), Nobu and Nikki Beach have all committed to the same four islands within two years. That’s the signal to watch, more than any single price per square foot.

( 02 ) Sales

Branded, beachfront and paid over time.

Nearly every launch is a branded residence with a hotel component, sold on plans that run from 50/50 to 80/20. That structure matters more than the headline price: a 60/40 plan on a Q4 2028 handover means most of your capital stays with you until the resort is already open.

The same brands cost a lot more per square foot in Dubai. The bet is whether that gap closes. We think it narrows, pushed by demand for furnished rentals near the resorts rather than by people buying to flip.

( 03 ) Rental

Rents are holding, and tenants are arriving before the towers are finished.

On the island, furnished one-bedrooms in our book sit between AED 65,000 and AED 90,000 a year; two-bedroom duplexes above that. Al Hamra Village remains the deepest rental market in the Emirate and the natural home for the resort workforce.

For owners: a unit handing over in Q4 2026 can be tenanted for a full year before Wynn opens. That is the window the earliest buyers are playing for.

( 04 ) Watchlist

Q4 2026: the first handover wave.

Three projects in the current book hand over this quarter and next: Manta Bay, Sora Beach Residences and Masa Residence. Watch how quickly they let, and at what furnished premium. That is the first real data the island will produce, and it will set the tone for the 2027–2028 launches.

Also watch: Mina Al Arab’s first branded product (Ritz-Carlton Residences, Porto Playa), and whether Downtown yields hold as supply arrives on the coast.

( Before you buy )

Is Ras Al Khaimah a good place to buy property?

It can be, for the right buyer. Foreigners can buy freehold in designated areas, there is no income or capital gains tax on UAE property, and AED 2M of property can qualify you for a Golden Visa. But 2026 price growth slowed, more than 34,000 new homes are expected by 2030, and nobody knows what Wynn will do to prices.

What published 2026 data shows

  • CBRE, H1 2026 (published 21 September 2026): Al Marjan Island apartment values up 23.1% year on year; CBRE says pricing and absorption moderated after the end of February 2026.
  • ValuStrat, Q2 2026 (reported 4 August 2026): the overall Ras Al Khaimah index up 5.4% on the year but down 0.5% on the quarter, the Emirate’s first quarterly decline in more than two years.
  • CBRE expects more than 34,000 residential units to be delivered in Ras Al Khaimah between 2026 and 2030, around 10,000 of them branded residences.

The two sources measure different things and should not be combined. Past price changes are not a forecast.

Who it suits

Start with one question: is the property to live in, to rent out or for a Golden Visa? Buyers with a clear answer, a budget that includes the fees, and the patience to check the developer, the payment plan and the handover date are the ones this market suits. If you are buying only because prices have risen, read the note above first.

For who can buy where, the fees and each step to the title deed, see buying property in Ras Al Khaimah as a foreigner.

What to check before you buy

  1. Confirm the rules. Rules and fees can change. Confirm them with the authority, or ask us, before you commit.
  2. Check the freehold status of the exact unit. Before you pay a deposit, confirm that the plot or building sits inside a designated freehold area.
  3. Check the developer, the plan and the plot. Look at the developer’s track record, the payment plan and the exact plot before you sign.
  4. Get the payment schedule in writing. For off-plan, ask for the SPA schedule, work out how much you pay before handover, and ask what happens if the handover date slips.
  5. Ask for the service charge. Running costs vary from building to building. Get the estimate in writing and include it in any yield sum.
  6. Look for evidence of rental demand. Ask for rents achieved in completed buildings nearby, not a projected yield, and check how much competing supply hands over in the same quarter.
  7. Budget the total cost. Add the 2% registration fee, AED 200 for plan issuance, AED 200 for the title deed, agency commission, furnishing and any developer charges.

Is Ras Al Khaimah a good place to buy property?

Often, yes: freehold ownership for foreigners, no property tax, a Golden Visa route from AED 2M, and limited beachfront supply. Growth has been slower than Dubai and new supply is arriving, so it suits buyers who check the details rather than following the story alone.

Does buying property in Ras Al Khaimah give you a Golden Visa?

It can. Property worth AED 2M or more can qualify you for a UAE Golden Visa, subject to the federal ICP rules at the time you apply. Official pages differ on whether the property route gives 5 or 10 years: ICP and RAKEZ describe 10 years, while the u.ae portal lists 5 years for real estate investments. RFR does not issue visas or give immigration advice.

What are the risks of buying property in Ras Al Khaimah?

Slower price growth than earlier in 2026, a large supply pipeline (more than 34,000 units expected between 2026 and 2030, per CBRE), handover dates that can slip, service charges that vary by building, and rental demand around the new resort that is not yet known. Confirm the freehold status of the exact unit and the current rules with the authority before you pay a deposit.

Is Ras Al Khaimah property a good investment in 2026?

Nobody can promise a return. CBRE reported Al Marjan Island apartment values up 23.1% year on year in H1 2026, and ValuStrat put the overall Ras Al Khaimah index up 5.4% on the year in Q2 2026, but both sources show growth slowing during 2026. Past price changes are not a forecast. Check the developer, payment plan, handover date, service charge and evidence of rental demand for the specific unit.
