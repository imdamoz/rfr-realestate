Can you get a UAE Golden Visa by buying property in Ras Al Khaimah?

By the RFR Advisory team · Published 24 November 2026. Golden Visa rules as published by ICP and the UAE Government portal u.ae, checked on 24 November 2026. Sources.

Short answer

Yes. The UAE Golden Visa (ICP calls it the Golden Residency) is federal, so it applies to property in Ras Al Khaimah too. You need to own one or more UAE properties worth a total of at least AED 2,000,000, registered in your name (a loan from an approved local bank is allowed), or off-plan units totalling at least AED 2,000,000 bought from an approved local developer, plus health insurance for you and your family. RFR helps you find qualifying property; ICP issues the visa.

( 01 ) Eligibility

Who qualifies through property.

ICP sets one figure for real estate investors: property in the UAE with a total value of at least AED 2,000,000. The rule is the same in every emirate, so property in Ras Al Khaimah counts.

Can the property be mortgaged?

Yes, according to ICP: the property may be financed through a loan, provided the financing comes from an approved local bank. The property must still be registered in your name.

Off-plan, or more than one property?

Both can count. ICP’s wording is “one or more” properties or off-plan units, so it is the total value that has to reach AED 2,000,000. Off-plan units must be bought from an approved local real estate company; ask the developer, and confirm with ICP which proof of purchase it accepts for a unit that is not yet handed over. ( 02 ) Term

Is it 5 or 10 years?

Official pages differ, so we show both. ICP, the authority that issues the visa, lists 10 years for real estate investors.

What each official page says

Official pageTerm listed for property investorsPage last updated
ICP Golden Residency Guide10 years for investors in the UAE’s real estate sector10 September 2026
UAE Government portal (u.ae), Golden visa page5 years for real estate investments28 July 2026

ICP’s service page for real estate investors also describes the Golden Residency as being for 10 years, and the ICP guide describes it as renewing automatically. The permit ICP issues states its own validity, so confirm the term with ICP when you apply.

( 03 ) Benefits

What the Golden Visa gives you.

The benefits below are the ones listed in the ICP Golden Residency Guide.

Benefits listed by ICP

  • Long-term residency without the need for a sponsor.
  • Residence permits for your spouse and children, regardless of the children’s age, and for your parents.
  • The freedom to live, work, study and invest in the UAE.
  • The ability to sponsor domestic workers, in line with UAE regulations and procedures.
  • If you are outside the UAE: a six-month, multiple-entry entry visa, renewable for a further six months, to complete the Golden Residency procedures.
  • If the Golden Residency holder dies, sponsored family members may stay in the UAE for the validity of their residence permits.

( 04 ) The process

How to get a Golden Visa through property in Ras Al Khaimah, step by step.

Five steps: buy and register property worth AED 2,000,000 or more in total, get the land registration letter and health insurance, then apply to ICP.

The five steps

What ICP asks for

For its service “Issue entry permit for residence of a real estate investor”, ICP lists:

  • Passport, valid for at least 6 months
  • Personal photo
  • A letter from the land registration department of the emirate confirming that you own one or more properties with a total value of at least AED 2,000,000
  • Health insurance valid inside the UAE
  • The property must be fully owned by the investor

ICP’s listed fees

Application feeAED 100
Issue feeAED 100
Smart services feeAED 100

These are the fees ICP lists for that entry-permit service only. Fees for the residence itself and the Emirates ID are set by ICP at the time you apply. The cost of buying the property is separate: what it costs to buy in Ras Al Khaimah.
