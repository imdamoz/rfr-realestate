Short answer

Al Marjan Island is a group of four man-made, coral-shaped islands off the Al Hamra coast in Ras Al Khaimah, where the Wynn resort is due to open in 2027. It is a freehold area, so foreign buyers can own outright. Studios start from about AED 920K, one-bedrooms rent for about AED 65–90K a year, and indicative gross yields are 6–8%.

( Who and how much )

Who it suits, and what it costs.

Supply is finite by design: the islands are fully master-planned, so the current launch wave is most of what will ever exist here.

Who it suits

Overseas investors, holiday-let owners, resort professionals and second-home buyers from Dubai: buyers who want a resort-led, branded home near the Wynn resort. Most of Ras Al Khaimah’s branded residences are being built here, including Address, Nobu, JW Marriott, Nikki Beach and W.

Holiday lets, furnished homes rented short-term to visitors, are common on Al Marjan Island near the resorts. If you want tenants in place from day one, or a quieter family community, compare the best areas to live and buy in Ras Al Khaimah: Al Hamra Village and Mina Al Arab are the most family-led.

Prices and rents

Studios start from about AED 920K, and one- and two-bedroom branded homes run about AED 1.7–3.5M. The nine off-plan launches in our book have developer entry prices from AED 920,000 to AED 4.06M, on plans from 50/50 to 80/20.

Furnished one-bedrooms in our book rent for between AED 65,000 and AED 90,000 a year; two-bedrooms for about AED 75–130K. Indicative gross yields are 6–8%. A unit handing over in Q4 2026 can be tenanted for a full year before Wynn opens.

CBRE, H1 2026 (published 21 September 2026): Al Marjan Island apartment values up 23.1% year on year; CBRE says pricing and absorption moderated after the end of February 2026. Past price changes are not a forecast, and nobody knows yet what prices or rents will do once Wynn opens. More in our market note.

( FAQ )

Al Marjan Island questions.

Where is Al Marjan Island?

In Ras Al Khaimah, UAE: a group of four man-made, coral-shaped islands off the Al Hamra coast. It is about 35 minutes from RAK Airport and 55 minutes from Dubai airport (DXB).

Can foreigners buy property on Al Marjan Island?

Yes. Al Marjan Island is a designated freehold area, so foreign buyers can own property there outright, including off-plan units.

How much does an apartment on Al Marjan Island cost?

Studios start from about AED 920K, and one- and two-bedroom branded homes run about AED 1.7–3.5M. The off-plan launches in our book start from AED 920,000 to AED 4.06M. Ranges are indicative, not a valuation; we send exact figures for any unit.

What do apartments on Al Marjan Island rent for?

One-bedrooms rent for about AED 65–90K a year and two-bedrooms for about AED 75–130K a year. Indicative gross yields are 6–8%.

When does Wynn Al Marjan Island open?

Wynn Al Marjan Island is scheduled to open in 2027. Almost every current launch on the island is priced against that date.
