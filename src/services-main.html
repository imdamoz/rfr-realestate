( Glossary )

Property terms, plainly.

Freehold

Full, outright ownership of a property, available to foreign buyers in the designated freehold areas, including Al Marjan Island, Mina Al Arab and Al Hamra Village; in Downtown RAK, only in designated projects such as Julphar Towers and RAK Central.

Off-plan

Buying a home before it is built, from renders and floor plans, usually paid in instalments as construction progresses.

Payment plan

The instalment schedule for an off-plan purchase, for example 60/40, meaning 60% paid during construction and 40% on or after handover.

Handover

The date a developer hands over keys to the buyer, when the balance payment is usually due.

Golden Visa

A long-term UAE residency visa. Property worth AED 2 million or more can qualify, subject to the federal ICP rules at the time you apply.

Gross yield

Annual rent divided by purchase price, before costs like service charges and management fees.

Net yield

Annual rent minus running costs, divided by purchase price: a more realistic return figure than gross yield.

Service charge

The annual fee owners pay for the upkeep of shared building and community facilities.

Title deed

The government-issued document proving ownership of a property, registered with the RAK Land Department.

NOC (no-objection certificate)

A certificate from the developer confirming no debts are owed on a unit, required before a resale can transfer.

Agency commission

The fee paid to an agency for a sale or lease, typically around 2% for sales and 5% for leasing in Ras Al Khaimah, plus VAT.

Branded residence

A home managed under a hotel or hospitality brand, such as Nobu or Marriott, often with access to hotel-style services.

Rental pool

A scheme where owners in a building share rental income from a shared booking and management system, rather than letting individually.

Registration fee

A government fee paid to the RAK Land Department when a property sale is registered, on top of the purchase price.

Resale

A property bought from its current owner rather than directly from the developer, as opposed to an off-plan or first-hand purchase.

Holiday let

A furnished home rented out short-term to visitors, common on Al Marjan Island near the resorts.
