( 02 ) Who can buy

Who can buy a villa in Ras Al Khaimah?

Foreigners can, in the designated freehold areas. Every area in the table above is one.

Foreign buyers and freehold

Foreigners can buy freehold property in Ras Al Khaimah’s designated freehold areas. Ras Al Khaimah Municipality lists them as Al Hamra Village, Mina Al Arab, Al Marjan Island, RAK Central, Julphar Towers, Al Maareedh (plots) and the upcoming Beach District. You own the home outright, on a title deed. UAE and GCC nationals can buy anywhere in the Emirate.

Check the exact plot or building is inside a freehold area before you pay a deposit. The full rules, fees and steps are in our guide can foreigners buy property in Ras Al Khaimah?

( 03 ) Golden Visa

Villas and the Golden Visa.

The UAE Golden Visa is federal, so a villa in Ras Al Khaimah counts towards it like any other UAE property.

What ICP asks for

The UAE Golden Visa (ICP calls it the Golden Residency) is federal, so it applies to property in Ras Al Khaimah too. You need to own one or more UAE properties worth a total of at least AED 2,000,000, registered in your name (a loan from an approved local bank is allowed), or off-plan units totalling at least AED 2,000,000 bought from an approved local developer, plus health insurance for you and your family. RFR helps you find qualifying property; ICP issues the visa.

RFR does not issue visas or give legal or immigration advice. The requirements, term, benefits and steps, with the official sources, are in our guide to the Golden Visa through property in Ras Al Khaimah.

( 05 ) How we help

How RFR helps you buy a villa.

From first call to keys. RFR handles the shortlist, checks, negotiation and paperwork.

If you’re buying

  1. Brief and shortlist. Budget, purpose and timing. Plenty of what we sell never gets listed, so tell us what you want and we shortlist homes worth your time, and say which to skip.
  2. Viewings and checks. In person, or on video from abroad. We check the developer, the service charge and the title before you commit.
  3. Offer and paperwork. We negotiate the price, then the sale agreement (an MOU for a resale, the developer’s SPA for off-plan) and the developer’s no-objection certificate.
  4. Transfer and keys. Registration at RAK Municipality and the title deed, then handover. The same advisor stays with you afterwards.
