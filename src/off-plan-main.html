What “off-plan” means

You’re buying something that isn’t built yet.

Off-plan means signing for a home before it’s finished. Building work may have started, but you can’t walk through your unit, so you decide from renders, floor plans, virtual tours and a visit to the site.

That’s why it helps to have someone check the developer’s track record, the payment plan and the exact plot before you sign. Weighing off-plan against a ready home? Our investor guide compares off-plan and ready property in Ras Al Khaimah.

Common questions.

What does off-plan mean?

Off-plan means signing for a home before it’s finished. You decide from renders, floor plans and a site visit, and pay in instalments as it’s built.

What off-plan projects are there in Ras Al Khaimah?

Nine current launches, all on Al Marjan Island, from developers including Emaar, Aldar, Ellington and Marriott, with starting prices from AED 920,000 to AED 4.06M.

Can foreigners buy off-plan on Al Marjan Island?

Yes. Al Marjan Island is freehold, so foreign buyers can own off-plan units outright, the same as ready homes.

What payment plans do off-plan projects in Ras Al Khaimah offer?

Current launches run from 50/50 to 80/20, meaning 50% to 80% is paid during construction and the rest on or after handover.

When do the off-plan launches hand over?

Handovers in our current book range from Q4 2026 (Manta Bay, Sora Beach Residences, Masa Residence) to 2028 (Address Residences, Nikki Beach Residences, Costa Mare).

Do starting prices change with the floor, view and payment plan?

Yes. Starting prices are the lowest entry point; floor, view and the payment plan chosen all move the final figure. We send exact unit-level pricing on request.
