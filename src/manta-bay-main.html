Where is Manta Bay?

Manta Bay is on Al Marjan Island in Ras Al Khaimah, a group of four man-made, coral-shaped islands off the Al Hamra coast. It is in Ras Al Khaimah, not Dubai: Dubai airport (DXB) is about 55 minutes from the island, RAK Airport about 35 minutes and Al Hamra Marina about 12 minutes.

The Wynn Al Marjan Island resort is being built on the same islands and is scheduled to open in 2027. See how the island compares in our guide to the best areas to live and buy in Ras Al Khaimah.

Who is the developer of Manta Bay?

Major Developers. Manta Bay is one of nine off-plan projects in Ras Al Khaimah that RFR Real Estate currently lists, all of them on Al Marjan Island.

With any off-plan purchase, check the developer’s track record, the payment plan and the exact plot before you sign. We do that with you.

What is the Manta Bay payment plan?

50/50: half of the price is paid during construction and the rest on or after handover. Plans on the launches we list run from 50/50 to 80/20, so Manta Bay asks for the smallest share before handover, alongside W Hotel & Residences.

Ask for the SPA payment schedule in writing, work out how much you pay before handover and ask what happens if the date moves. All plans side by side: off-plan payment plans in Ras Al Khaimah.

What unit types are available at Manta Bay?

Studios to two-bedrooms. The unit we currently list is a furnished, sea-view studio with 1 bath and 480 sq ft, at AED 1,200,000.

Availability changes quickly on a launch this close to handover, so ask us what’s left and we’ll send current floor plans and unit-level prices.

How much does Manta Bay cost?

Starting prices are from AED 1.2M, the second-lowest starting price of the nine launches in our book, after Masa Residence. On top of the price, the buyer pays a 2% registration fee, plus AED 200 for plan issuance and AED 200 for the title deed.

Fees and the steps to the title deed are in our Ras Al Khaimah property investment guide.

When does Manta Bay hand over?

Q4 2026, the earliest handover in our current off-plan book. Sora Beach Residences and Masa Residence are due in the same quarter; the other six launches hand over between Q4 2027 and Q4 2028.

Handover dates can slip, so ask what happens if the date moves before you sign.

Is Manta Bay a good investment?

It depends on what you want the home to do, and nobody can promise a return. Here is the case for, the case against and what to check.

  • The case for. Manta Bay has the earliest handover in our off-plan book, and a unit handing over in Q4 2026 can be tenanted for a full year before the Wynn resort opens, scheduled for 2027. Its starting price is the second lowest of the nine launches, the 50/50 plan leaves half the price until handover, and Al Marjan Island is freehold, so foreign buyers can own outright.
  • The case against. Sora Beach Residences and Masa Residence hand over in the same quarter, so new homes will be competing for tenants at the same time, and nobody knows yet how quickly they will let or what prices and rents will do once Wynn opens. CBRE expects more than 34,000 homes to be delivered across the Emirate between 2026 and 2030, and published data show price growth slowing during 2026.
  • What the area shows. Furnished one-bedrooms in our book on Al Marjan Island rent for between AED 65,000 and AED 90,000 a year, and indicative gross yields for the island are 6–8%. Those are area ranges before costs, not figures for Manta Bay.
  • What to check. The SPA payment schedule, the service charge in writing, rents achieved in completed buildings nearby rather than a projected yield, and the total cost including the 2% registration fee, furnishing and any developer charges.
