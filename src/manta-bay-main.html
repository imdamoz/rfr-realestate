Short answer

Manta Bay is an off-plan launch by Major Developers on Al Marjan Island, in Ras Al Khaimah, about 55 minutes from Dubai airport (DXB). Homes run from studios to two-bedrooms, starting from AED 1.2M on a 50/50 payment plan, with handover in Q4 2026: the earliest handover of the nine launches in our off-plan book, so you can let it before the Wynn resort opens in 2027. We currently list a studio there at AED 1,200,000.

Al Marjan Island is a designated freehold area, so foreign buyers can own off-plan units there outright. Starting prices are the lowest entry point; floor, view and payment plan move the final figure, and we send exact unit-level pricing on request. New to buying here? ( FAQ )

Manta Bay questions.

Where is Manta Bay?

On Al Marjan Island in Ras Al Khaimah, a group of four man-made islands off the Al Hamra coast. It is in Ras Al Khaimah, not Dubai; Dubai airport (DXB) is about 55 minutes from the island.

Who is the developer of Manta Bay?

Major Developers.

How much does a home at Manta Bay cost?

Starting prices are from AED 1.2M, and the studio we currently list there is AED 1,200,000. Floor, view and payment plan move the final figure; we send exact unit-level pricing on request.

When does Manta Bay hand over?

Q4 2026, the earliest handover of the nine launches in our current off-plan book, so you can let it before the Wynn resort opens in 2027.

What is the Manta Bay payment plan?

50/50: half is paid during construction and the rest on or after handover.

What unit types are there at Manta Bay?

Studios to two-bedrooms. The unit we list is a furnished, sea-view studio with 1 bath and 480 sq ft.

Can foreigners buy at Manta Bay?

Yes. Al Marjan Island is freehold, so foreign buyers can own off-plan units there outright.
