( FAQ )

Common questions.

Can foreigners buy property in Ras Al Khaimah?

Yes. Foreign buyers can own freehold property outright in Al Marjan Island, Mina Al Arab, Al Hamra Village and Downtown RAK.

Does buying property in Ras Al Khaimah give me a Golden Visa?

It can. A property investment of AED 2M or more can qualify for the UAE’s 10-year Golden Visa, subject to the usual approval process.

What government fees do I pay when buying in Ras Al Khaimah?

A property registration fee is paid to the RAK Land Department on top of the purchase price. We confirm the exact figure for any specific unit before you commit.

Do I need to be in the UAE to buy?

No. We run viewings over video for buyers abroad and can manage the paperwork and the property once you own it.

What’s the difference between buying off-plan and buying a ready home?

Off-plan means paying in instalments for a home that isn’t finished yet, usually at a lower entry price. A ready home costs more upfront but you can view and move into it straight away.
