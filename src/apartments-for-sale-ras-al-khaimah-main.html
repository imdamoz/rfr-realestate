( 04 ) The process

How buying with RFR works, step by step.

From first call to keys. RFR handles the shortlist, checks, negotiation and paperwork.

If you’re buying

  1. Brief and shortlist. Budget, purpose and timing. We shortlist ready homes and launches worth your time, and say which to skip.
  2. Viewings and checks. In person, or on video from abroad. We check the developer, the service charge and the title before you commit.
  3. Offer and paperwork. We negotiate the price or the payment plan, then the sale agreement (an MOU for a resale, the developer’s SPA for off-plan) and the developer’s no-objection certificate.
  4. Transfer and keys. Registration at RAK Municipality and the title deed, then handover. The same advisor stays with you afterwards.

What it costs to buy
