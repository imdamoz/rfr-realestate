Short answer

Al Hamra Village is Ras Al Khaimah’s original master community, with an 18-hole championship golf course, a marina and yacht club, a mall and the deepest rental market in RAK. It is a freehold area, so foreign buyers can own outright. Apartments start from about AED 500K, one-bedrooms rent for about AED 45–65K a year, and indicative gross yields are 7–8.5%.

( Who and how much )

Who it suits, and what it costs.

Closest established community to Al Marjan Island, so it’s where much of the resort workforce is expected to rent once Wynn opens in 2027.

Who it suits

Families, golfers, the Al Hamra and Wynn workforce, and investors who want tenants in place from day one. With Mina Al Arab it is one of the two most family-led areas we cover, with schools, villas and townhouses nearby, and it adds a golf course and marina.

If you want a resort-led, branded home near the Wynn resort, look at Al Marjan Island instead; for a quieter lagoon community, read the Mina Al Arab area guide. Compare the best areas to live and buy in Ras Al Khaimah.

Prices and rents

Apartments start from about AED 500K, and townhouses and villas run about AED 1.5–5M. One-bedrooms rent for about AED 45–65K a year and three-bedroom villas for about AED 110–160K.

Indicative gross yields are 7–8.5%. With Downtown RAK, Al Hamra Village shows the highest indicative gross yields in our book, ahead of Al Marjan Island and Mina Al Arab. The furnished one-bedroom we list on Falcon Island rents for AED 65,500 a year.

Buyers typically pay a property registration fee to the RAK Land Department on top of the purchase price. Fees, the steps to the title deed and the Golden Visa are in our guide to investing in Ras Al Khaimah property.

Al Hamra Village questions.

Where is Al Hamra Village?

On the Al Hamra coast of Ras Al Khaimah, UAE. It is the closest established community to Al Marjan Island, about 12 minutes away, and about 50 minutes from Dubai airport (DXB).

Can foreigners buy property in Al Hamra Village?

Yes. Al Hamra Village is one of the designated freehold areas listed by Ras Al Khaimah Municipality, so foreign buyers can own property there outright. How buying works for foreigners.
