Short answer

It depends on how you’ll use the home. Al Marjan Island suits buyers who want a resort-led, branded home near the Wynn resort, due to open in 2027; Al Hamra Village suits families, golfers and investors who want tenants in place from day one; and Mina Al Arab suits families who want a quieter, low-rise lagoon community. For off-plan, Manta Bay is a launch on Al Marjan Island with studios to two-bedrooms, handing over in Q4 2026. Downtown RAK, the working city, suits professionals who want short commutes and the lowest entry prices. Al Marjan Island, Al Hamra Village and Mina Al Arab are designated freehold areas, so foreign buyers can own property there outright.

( In short )

The best areas to live in Ras Al Khaimah.

It depends on how you’ll use the home. Each of the four in a few sentences; the figures are indicative ranges based on our own listings and public launch data.

Living in Al Marjan Island

Al Marjan Island is a group of four man-made, coral-shaped islands off the Al Hamra coast, where most of Ras Al Khaimah’s branded residences are being built and where the Wynn resort is due to open in 2027. It suits overseas investors, holiday-let owners, resort professionals and second-home buyers from Dubai. Studios start from about AED 920K, one-bedrooms rent for about AED 65–90K a year, and indicative gross yields are 6–8%.

Living in Mina Al Arab

Mina Al Arab is a low-rise waterfront community of villas, townhouses and lagoon-front apartments, built around lagoons and protected mangroves and anchored by the Anantara and InterContinental resorts. It suits families relocating to RAK, long-term residents and buyers who want water without the resort crowds; RAK Academy is about 10 minutes away. Apartments start from about AED 800K, a three-bedroom villa rents for about AED 85–140K a year, and indicative gross yields are 5.5–7%.

Living in Al Hamra Village

Al Hamra Village is the Emirate’s original master community, with an 18-hole championship golf course, a marina and yacht club, a mall and the deepest rental market in RAK. It suits families, golfers and investors who want tenants in place from day one, and it is the closest established community to Al Marjan Island. Apartments start from about AED 500K, a one-bedroom rents for about AED 45–65K a year, and indicative gross yields are 7–8.5%.

Living in Downtown RAK

Downtown RAK is the working city around Al Nakheel, Julphar and the corniche, with the shortest commutes to RAKEZ, the hospitals and government. It suits professionals, RAKEZ business owners and medical and education staff. Apartments start from about AED 400K, the lowest entry price of the four, a two-bedroom rents for about AED 50–95K a year, and indicative gross yields are 7–9%.

Al Marjan Island, Mina Al Arab and Al Hamra Village are designated freehold areas, so foreign buyers can own property there. In Downtown RAK, freehold is limited to designated projects such as Julphar Towers and RAK Central. Ranges are indicative, not a valuation; we send exact figures for any unit. ( FAQ )

Common questions.

What are the best areas to live in Ras Al Khaimah?

It depends on how you’ll use the home. Al Hamra Village suits families, golfers and investors who want tenants in place; Mina Al Arab suits families who want a quieter, low-rise lagoon community; Al Marjan Island suits buyers who want a resort-led, branded home near the Wynn resort; and Downtown RAK suits professionals who want short commutes and the lowest entry prices. RFR covers all four.

Which area of Ras Al Khaimah is best for property investment?

It depends on your goal. Al Hamra Village suits investors who want tenants in place from day one, and has the deepest rental market in RAK. Al Marjan Island suits resort-led off-plan and holiday lets, and all nine off-plan launches in our book, including Manta Bay, are there. Mina Al Arab suits buyers who want space in a family-led community, and Downtown RAK has the lowest entry prices. More in our Ras Al Khaimah property investment guide.

Can foreigners buy in all these areas?

Not all of them. Al Marjan Island (including Manta Bay), Al Hamra Village and Mina Al Arab are designated freehold areas, so foreign buyers can own property there outright. In Downtown RAK, freehold is limited to designated projects such as Julphar Towers and RAK Central. UAE and GCC nationals can buy anywhere in the Emirate. Full answer: can foreigners buy property in Ras Al Khaimah?

Is Ras Al Khaimah a good place to buy property?

It can be, for the right buyer. Foreigners can buy freehold in designated areas, there is no income or capital gains tax on UAE property, and AED 2M of property can qualify you for a Golden Visa. But 2026 price growth slowed, more than 34,000 new homes are expected by 2030, and nobody knows what Wynn will do to prices. Read our Ras Al Khaimah property investment guide.

Can foreigners buy property in Ras Al Khaimah?

Yes, in the designated freehold areas. Al Marjan Island, Mina Al Arab and Al Hamra Village are all freehold, so foreign buyers can own property there outright. In Downtown RAK, freehold is limited to designated projects such as Julphar Towers and RAK Central, so check the exact building before you pay a deposit.

Which area of Ras Al Khaimah has the highest rental yield?

Downtown RAK and Al Hamra Village show the highest indicative gross yields in our book, around 7–9%, ahead of Al Marjan Island and Mina Al Arab.

Where is the cheapest place to buy an apartment in Ras Al Khaimah?

Downtown RAK has the lowest entry prices in our book, with apartments from around AED 400,000.

Which area suits families?

Mina Al Arab and Al Hamra Village are the most family-led, with schools, villas and townhouses nearby; Al Hamra Village adds a golf course and marina.

Which area is closest to Wynn Al Marjan Island?

Al Marjan Island itself is where Wynn is being built. Al Hamra Village is the closest established community, around 12 minutes away, and is where much of the resort workforce is expected to rent.

What are the government fees when you buy in Ras Al Khaimah?

Buyers typically pay a property registration fee to the RAK Land Department on top of the purchase price. We confirm the exact figure for any specific unit before you commit.
