Where is Mina Al Arab?

Mina Al Arab is on the coast of Ras Al Khaimah, one of the seven emirates of the UAE. It is a waterfront community built around lagoons and protected mangroves, with a mangrove beach and boardwalks, and it is anchored by the Anantara Mina Al Arab and InterContinental Mina Al Arab resorts.

RAK Academy is about 10 minutes away, RAK city centre about 15 minutes and RAK Airport about 30 minutes. See it next to the other areas.

Property types in Mina Al Arab

Villas, townhouses and lagoon-front apartments, in an established, low-rise, family-led neighbourhood. We also list a residential plot there now.

Apartments start from about AED 800K and villas run about AED 2.5–6M. A three-bedroom villa rents for about AED 85–140K a year and a two-bedroom apartment for about AED 60–95K. Indicative gross yields are 5.5–7%.

The Ritz-Carlton Residences and Porto Playa are the next phase: the first branded product on this side of the coast.
