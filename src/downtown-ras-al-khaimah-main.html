( Who and how much )

Who it suits, and what it costs.

Less glamour, more cash flow. Downtown RAK is where we send clients who care about net yield over sea view.

Who it suits

Professionals, RAKEZ business owners, and medical and education staff who want short commutes: Downtown RAK has the shortest commutes to RAKEZ, the hospitals and government of the four areas we cover. It also suits investors who put yield first; when we match a goal to an area, yield points to Downtown RAK or Al Hamra Village.

If you want a resort-led, branded home near the Wynn resort, look at the Al Marjan Island area guide; for a family-led community with villas and townhouses, read about Al Hamra Village or Mina Al Arab.

Prices and rents

Buyers typically pay a property registration fee to the RAK Land Department on top of the purchase price. Fees and the steps to the title deed are in our guide to investing in Ras Al Khaimah property, and rents across the city are on apartments for rent in Ras Al Khaimah.

( Who can buy )

Freehold in Downtown RAK, and who can buy.

Unlike Al Marjan Island, Al Hamra Village and Mina Al Arab, Downtown RAK is not freehold as a whole.

Where foreigners can own

In Downtown RAK, freehold is limited to designated projects such as Julphar Towers and RAK Central. Ras Al Khaimah Municipality lists the freehold areas as Al Hamra Village, Mina Al Arab, Al Marjan Island, RAK Central, Julphar Towers, Al Maareedh (plots) and the upcoming Beach District. UAE and GCC nationals can buy anywhere in the Emirate.

Before you pay a deposit

Freehold means full ownership in your own name, recorded on a title deed. Confirm that the exact building sits inside a freehold area before you pay a deposit; outside the freehold areas, ask in writing what ownership is on offer. Full answer: can foreigners buy property in Ras Al Khaimah?
