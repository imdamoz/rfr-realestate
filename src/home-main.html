      Who is RFR Real Estate?

      RFR Real Estate & Advisory FZ-LLC is a licensed real estate brokerage and advisory firm based in Ras Al Khaimah, UAE. We help clients buy, sell, rent, let and manage homes, including off-plan launches. About RFR Real Estate.

      Is rfrrealestate.com the official website of RFR Real Estate?

      Yes. www.rfrrealestate.com is the only official website for RFR Real Estate & Advisory FZ-LLC in Ras Al Khaimah. We don’t use any other domain.

      Which areas do you cover?

      Mainly Al Marjan Island, Mina Al Arab, Al Hamra Village and Downtown RAK. We don’t work outside Ras Al Khaimah.

      Can you help if I’m buying from abroad?

      Yes, many of our clients live overseas. We view properties for you on video and can manage the place once you own it. Foreign buyers can own freehold in the designated freehold areas: see can foreigners buy property in Ras Al Khaimah?

      Is Ras Al Khaimah a good area for property investment?

      Often, yes. Prices are well below Dubai, Wynn opens in 2027 and beachfront is limited. We’ll tell you if it doesn’t suit you.

      How do I get started?

      Call, WhatsApp or email. It’s free, there’s no commitment, and we’ll suggest a next step.
