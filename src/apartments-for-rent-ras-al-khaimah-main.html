( 03 ) The process

How renting with RFR works, step by step.

Looking for a home? Send your budget and move-in date and we’ll line up viewings this week.

If you’re renting

  1. Budget and area. Tell us the rent, the size and where. We shortlist only homes that are actually available.
  2. Viewings this week. In person or on video. Furnished island units move fast.
  3. Contract and cheques. Most landlords here accept up to 4 cheques a year.
  4. Keys. Move-in, utilities and one contact for anything after.
