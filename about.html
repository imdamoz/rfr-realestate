( FAQ )

Common questions.

Which areas do you cover?

Mainly Al Marjan Island, Mina Al Arab, Al Hamra Village and Downtown RAK. We don’t work outside Ras Al Khaimah.

Do you work with both buyers and tenants?

Yes, and owners too. You keep the same contact whatever you need.

Can you help if I’m buying from abroad?

Yes, many of our clients live overseas. We view properties for you on video and can manage the place once you own it.

Is Ras Al Khaimah a good area for property investment?

Often, yes. Prices are well below Dubai, Wynn opens in 2027 and beachfront is limited. We’ll tell you if it doesn’t suit you.

How do you screen tenants?

We check ID, visa, income and past landlord references. You approve every tenant.

How do I get started?

Call, WhatsApp or email. It’s free, there’s no commitment, and we’ll suggest a next step.

Have a property in mind?

Send us a message.

A line or two is plenty. An advisor will reply the same day with a clear next step.
