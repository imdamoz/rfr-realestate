Al Marjan Island vs Al Hamra Village vs Mina Al Arab: which Ras Al Khaimah area suits you?

Three coastal communities, compared on the facts we publish.

BY THE RFR ADVISORY TEAM · PUBLISHED 16 NOVEMBER 2026 · UPDATED 16 NOVEMBER 2026

Al Marjan Island suits buyers who want resort-led, off-plan branded residences: all nine launches in RFR’s book are there, from AED 920,000, and the Wynn resort is due to open in 2027. Al Hamra Village, the Emirate’s original master community with golf and a marina, has the deepest rental market in RAK and the highest indicative gross yield of the three, at about 7–8.5%. Mina Al Arab is a low-rise lagoon and mangrove community of villas, townhouses and lagoon-front apartments, home to families relocating to RAK, long-term residents and buyers who want water without the resort crowds.

This page puts side by side what we already publish on our area pages, area guides, listings and off-plan pages. It adds no new figures. Price, rent and yield ranges are indicative, based on RFR listings and public launch data.

( 02 ) BEST FOR

Which area suits which buyer?

Our buying guide suggests matching your goal to the area: resort-led off-plan on Al Marjan Island, yield in Al Hamra Village (or Downtown RAK) and space in Mina Al Arab. This is what our pages say about each.

Al Marjan Island

  • Resort-led off-plan. Most of RAK’s branded residences are being built here, including Address, Nobu, JW Marriott, Nikki Beach and W.
  • Being near the Wynn resort, which is due to open in 2027.
  • Overseas investors, holiday-let owners, resort professionals and second-home buyers from Dubai, the residents our area page lists.
  • An earlier handover. Manta Bay, Masa Residence and Sora Beach Residences are listed for Q4 2026; see all launches compared.
  • RFR’s advisory view: supply is finite by design. The islands are fully master-planned, so the current launch wave is most of what will ever exist here.

Al Hamra Village

  • Yield. About 7–8.5% gross (indicative), the highest range of these three areas.
  • Tenants in place. It has the deepest rental market in RAK and a decade of tenants.
  • Golf and marina life: an 18-hole championship golf course, Al Hamra Marina & Yacht Club, Al Hamra Mall and the Waldorf Astoria.
  • The lowest entry price of the three, with apartments from about AED 500K (indicative).
  • RFR’s advisory view: it is the closest established community to Al Marjan Island, so it’s where the resort staff arriving in 2027 will rent.

Mina Al Arab

  • Space. Villas, townhouses and lagoon-front apartments in a low-rise, established, family-led neighbourhood.
  • Families relocating to RAK and long-term residents. RAK Academy is about 10 minutes away and RAK city centre about 15.
  • Water without the resort crowds: lagoons, protected mangroves, a mangrove beach and boardwalks.
  • Land. RFR lists a residential plot near the community pool, one of the last left in its phase.
  • RFR’s advisory view: The Ritz-Carlton Residences and Porto Playa are the next phase, the first branded product on this side of the coast.

( 04 ) IN COMMON

What the three areas have in common

  • Freehold. All three are designated freehold areas listed by Ras Al Khaimah Municipality, so foreign buyers can own property in each. UAE and GCC nationals can buy anywhere in the Emirate.
  • The same government fees. As set out in our buying guide, the buyer pays a registration fee of 2% of the market value, plus AED 200 for plan issuance and AED 200 for the title deed. Agency commission and developer charges are separate.
  • The Golden Visa. Property worth AED 2M or more can qualify you for a UAE Golden Visa, subject to the federal ICP rules at the time you apply. Read Golden Visa via RAK property first.
  • One advisor. RFR covers all three areas, and Downtown RAK. One advisor stays with you from the first call through to handover and after.

( 05 ) FAQ

Common questions

Which is cheapest to buy into: Al Marjan Island, Al Hamra Village or Mina Al Arab?

On RFR’s indicative ranges, Al Hamra Village has the lowest entry point, with apartments from about AED 500K, then Mina Al Arab, with apartments from about AED 800K, then Al Marjan Island, with studios from about AED 920K. The ranges are based on RFR listings and public launch data, not a valuation.

Which area has the highest rental yield?

On our indicative ranges, Al Hamra Village is highest of the three at about 7–8.5% gross, then Al Marjan Island at about 6–8% and Mina Al Arab at about 5.5–7%. These are gross figures, not a forecast or a promise of returns. Ask RFR for the figures on a specific unit.

Where are the off-plan launches?

All nine off-plan launches in RFR’s current book are on Al Marjan Island, with starting prices from AED 920,000 to AED 4,060,000. We list no off-plan launches in Al Hamra Village or Mina Al Arab. For Mina Al Arab, our advisory note names The Ritz-Carlton Residences and Porto Playa as the next phase, but we publish no prices or dates for it. See all nine launches compared.

Can foreigners buy property in all three areas?

Yes. Al Marjan Island, Al Hamra Village and Mina Al Arab are all designated freehold areas listed by Ras Al Khaimah Municipality, so foreign buyers can own property in each. UAE and GCC nationals can buy anywhere in the Emirate.

Which area suits families?

Our area pages list families among the residents of both Mina Al Arab and Al Hamra Village. Mina Al Arab is low-rise and built around lagoons and mangroves, with RAK Academy about 10 minutes away. Al Hamra Village has golf, a marina, a mall and the Waldorf Astoria. On Al Marjan Island, the residents we list are mainly overseas investors, holiday-let owners, resort professionals and second-home buyers from Dubai.

Which area is closest to the Wynn resort?

Wynn Al Marjan Island is being built on Al Marjan Island and is due to open in 2027. Al Hamra Village is the closest established community to Al Marjan Island; RFR’s advisory view is that it is where the resort staff arriving in 2027 will rent. That is our view, not a forecast of rents or prices.
