Can foreigners buy property in Ras Al Khaimah?

Who can buy, where, and what freehold means.

BY THE RFR ADVISORY TEAM · PUBLISHED 17 NOVEMBER 2026

Yes. Foreigners can buy freehold property in Ras Al Khaimah, but only in the Emirate’s designated freehold areas, which Ras Al Khaimah Municipality lists as Al Hamra Village, Mina Al Arab, Al Marjan Island, RAK Central, Julphar Towers, Al Maareedh (plots) and the upcoming Beach District. UAE and GCC nationals can buy anywhere in the Emirate.

This page answers the ownership question only. For the costs, documents and each step of a purchase, read our guide to buying property in Ras Al Khaimah. If a term is new to you, the Ras Al Khaimah property glossary explains it in plain English.

( 02 ) FREEHOLD

Freehold vs leasehold

Freehold means full ownership of a property in your own name, recorded on a title deed. It is the form of ownership foreign buyers get in Ras Al Khaimah’s designated freehold areas, and it is what every area guide on this site refers to.

We do not publish leasehold terms. Outside the designated freehold areas, freehold is not open to other foreign nationals, so if a unit you like sits outside them, ask the developer or RFR in writing what kind of ownership is on offer before you pay anything.

( 04 ) OFF-PLAN

Buying off-plan as a foreigner

Most new supply in Ras Al Khaimah is off-plan on Al Marjan Island, a designated freehold area. RFR’s current book has 9 launches there, with entry prices from AED 920K to AED 4.06M. Payment plans run from 50/50 to 80/20, so part of the price is paid during construction and the balance typically on handover.

( 05 ) GOLDEN VISA

Does buying give you a Golden Visa?

It can. Property worth AED 2M or more can qualify you for a UAE Golden Visa, subject to the federal ICP rules at the time you apply. Official pages currently differ on whether the property route gives 5 or 10 years, and RAKEZ states that the applicant must be inside the UAE when submitting the visa request. Our Golden Visa via Ras Al Khaimah property guide sets out what ICP, u.ae and RAKEZ each say. RFR does not issue visas or give immigration advice.
