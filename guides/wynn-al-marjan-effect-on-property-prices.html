Wynn Al Marjan and RAK property prices

What has been announced, what the 2026 data shows, and what nobody knows yet.

BY THE RFR ADVISORY TEAM · PUBLISHED 15 NOVEMBER 2026

Wynn Al Marjan Island is scheduled to open in September 2027. Published 2026 data show Ras Al Khaimah property values up year on year, fastest for apartments on Al Marjan Island, but growth slowed during the year. Nobody can tell you what prices will do once the resort opens.

This guide separates three things that are often blurred together in sales material: what has actually been announced about the resort, what published research shows about prices so far, and what is simply unknown. It ends with the questions we suggest you ask before buying, not a recommendation to buy.

General information only. This guide is not legal, financial, tax or immigration advice. Rules, fees and figures change, sometimes without notice; confirm anything you rely on with the official authority before you act. Past price changes are not a forecast of future prices. Nothing here is a recommendation to buy or sell, or a prediction that any property will rise in value because Wynn Al Marjan Island opens.

Two points stand out. First, both sources show year-on-year growth, strongest for Al Marjan Island apartments. Second, both show it slowing: CBRE says pricing and absorption moderated after the end of February 2026, and ValuStrat recorded the Emirate’s first quarterly decline in more than two years in Q2 2026, with Al Marjan apartment values flat on the quarter.

Neither source attributes a fixed share of these moves to the resort, and we do not either.

( 03 ) UNKNOWNS

What nobody can know yet

  • Post-opening prices. There is no published data on what happens after September 2027, because it has not happened. Anyone who promises a post-opening price move is guessing.
  • The opening date itself. Targets for very large projects can move. Plan around the possibility that it does.
  • Rental demand. A large resort brings staff and visitors, but how many will rent, where, and at what rent is not yet known.
  • Other drivers. Interest rates, the wider UAE market and supply elsewhere in the Emirate move prices too.
( 04 ) SUPPLY

The supply pipeline cuts both ways

CBRE expects more than 34,000 residential units to be delivered in Ras Al Khaimah between 2026 and 2030, around 10,000 of them branded residences. On Al Marjan, nearly every launch in RFR’s current book is a branded residence with a hotel component. If demand keeps pace, prices and rents can hold; if deliveries arrive faster than buyers and tenants, they can soften. Our September 2026 market note flags the Q4 2026 handover wave as the first real test of how quickly new island units let.

( 05 ) QUESTIONS TO ASK

Questions to ask before you buy

These are the questions we would ask on any unit sold on the Wynn story. They are prompts for your own due diligence, not investment advice.

  1. When does it hand over, compared with September 2027? A unit handing over before the resort opens can be let sooner; one handing over later keeps more of your cash until completion. Ask what happens under the SPA if the handover date slips.
  2. What is the payment plan? Plans in our book run from 50/50 to 80/20. Work out how much you pay before handover and how you will fund the balance.
  3. What will the service charge be? Running costs vary from building to building. Ask the developer for the estimated service charge in writing and include it in any yield sum.
  4. Who will rent it, and at what rent? Ask for evidence from completed buildings nearby, not a projected yield. ValuStrat put average gross yields at 5.3% in Q2 2026; your unit may be higher or lower.
  5. How much competing supply hands over at the same time? Check what else completes on the island in the same quarter.
  6. What are the total costs? Include the 2% registration fee, agency fees, furnishing and any developer charges. Our buying guide lists the government fees.
( 06 ) FAQ

Common questions

Will Wynn Al Marjan Island raise property prices in Ras Al Khaimah?

Nobody can promise that. Published data show values rose year on year in 2026, fastest for Al Marjan Island apartments, but growth slowed during the year, and there is no data yet on prices after the resort opens. More than 34,000 new units are also expected across the Emirate by 2030.

When does Wynn Al Marjan Island open?

It is scheduled to open in September 2027, according to Gulf News and Khaleej Times coverage of Wynn Resorts’ commentary and CBRE’s H1 2026 review. Dates for projects of this size can change.

How much is Wynn Al Marjan Island costing?

Press coverage of Wynn Resorts’ August 2026 earnings reported the budget rising by about USD 600 million to around USD 5.7 billion. CBRE’s H1 2026 review, published on 21 September 2026, still referred to USD 5.1 billion.

How much have Al Marjan Island prices risen?

CBRE reported Al Marjan Island apartment values up 23.1% year on year in H1 2026. ValuStrat reported them up 9.4% a year in Q2 2026, at AED 928,000 per unit or AED 1,160 per sq. ft., unchanged on the quarter. The methods differ, so the numbers should not be combined.

Should I buy on Al Marjan Island before Wynn opens?

That depends on your budget, timing and goals, and we cannot answer it in a general guide. Compare the handover date with the September 2027 opening target, the payment plan, the service charge, evidence of rental demand and competing supply before you decide.

How much new housing is coming to Ras Al Khaimah?

CBRE expects more than 34,000 residential units to be delivered between 2026 and 2030, around 10,000 of them branded residences. That supply can push prices and rents either way.
