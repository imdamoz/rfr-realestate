Is Ras Al Khaimah a good place to buy property?

The case for buying, the risks, and who it suits.

BY THE RFR ADVISORY TEAM · PUBLISHED 18 NOVEMBER 2026

It can be, for the right buyer. Foreigners can buy freehold in designated areas, there is no income or capital gains tax on UAE property, and AED 2M of property can qualify you for a Golden Visa. But 2026 price growth slowed, more than 34,000 new homes are expected by 2030, and nobody knows what Wynn will do to prices.

This guide brings together what RFR already publishes on ownership, the Wynn resort, off-plan launches, the Golden Visa and the three main coastal areas, so you can weigh the case for buying against the risks. It adds no new figures. If a term is new to you, the Ras Al Khaimah property glossary explains it in plain English.

( 02 ) OWNERSHIP

Can you own it as a foreigner?

Yes, in the designated freehold areas, which Ras Al Khaimah Municipality lists as Al Hamra Village, Mina Al Arab, Al Marjan Island, RAK Central, Julphar Towers, Al Maareedh (plots) and the upcoming Beach District. UAE and GCC nationals can buy anywhere in the Emirate. Freehold means full ownership in your own name, recorded on a title deed.

You do not necessarily need to be in the UAE to buy: RFR runs video viewings for overseas buyers, and a power of attorney can be used if someone acts for you at the transfer. Who can buy where is set out in can foreigners buy property in Ras Al Khaimah?, and the fees, documents and seven steps to transfer are in our guide to buying property in Ras Al Khaimah.

( 03 ) THE WYNN EFFECT

What Wynn Al Marjan Island does, and doesn’t, tell you

Wynn Al Marjan Island, an integrated resort on Al Marjan Island, is scheduled to open in September 2027, according to press coverage of Wynn Resorts’ commentary and CBRE’s H1 2026 review. Dates for projects of this size can change.

Published 2026 data show values rising, and slowing:

  • CBRE, H1 2026 (published 21 September 2026): Al Marjan Island apartment values up 23.1% year on year; CBRE says pricing and absorption moderated after the end of February 2026.
  • ValuStrat, Q2 2026 (reported 4 August 2026): the overall Ras Al Khaimah index up 5.4% on the year but down 0.5% on the quarter, the Emirate’s first quarterly decline in more than two years, with Al Marjan Island apartment values unchanged on the quarter.
  • CBRE expects more than 34,000 residential units to be delivered in Ras Al Khaimah between 2026 and 2030, around 10,000 of them branded residences. If deliveries arrive faster than buyers and tenants, prices and rents can soften.

The two sources measure different things and should not be combined, and neither attributes a share of these moves to the resort. Past price changes are not a forecast. Our guide to Wynn Al Marjan and RAK property prices sets out what has been announced, what the data show and what nobody can know yet.

( 04 ) OFF-PLAN

Off-plan launches and payment plans

Most new supply in Ras Al Khaimah is off-plan on Al Marjan Island. RFR’s current book has 9 launches there, each from a different developer, with starting prices from AED 920,000 (Masa Residence) to AED 4,060,000 (W Hotel & Residences). Payment plans run from 50/50 to 80/20, and five of the nine are 60/40. Listed handovers run from Q4 2026 to Q4 2028.

These are starting prices; the floor, view and payment plan change the final figure. For off-plan you sign the developer’s sale and purchase agreement (SPA) and pay against its schedule, and the SPA is what binds you. Our off-plan payment plans guide explains how the plans work, and the launches compared puts all nine in one table with developer, plan, handover and unit types.

( 05 ) GOLDEN VISA

The Golden Visa, with its caveat

Official UAE sources set the property route to a Golden Visa at property worth at least AED 2 million in total, completed or off-plan, owned in your name. They do not currently agree on how long the visa lasts: ICP and RAKEZ describe 10 years, while the u.ae portal’s eligibility table lists 5 years for real estate investments. RAKEZ states that the applicant must be inside the UAE when submitting the visa request.

If the visa is the goal, confirm the threshold, the term and the document list with ICP or the Ras Al Khaimah channel you will apply through before you buy. Our Golden Visa via Ras Al Khaimah property guide sets out what each official page says. RFR does not issue visas or give immigration advice.

( 07 ) BEFORE YOU BUY

Risks, and what to check before you buy

These are general prompts drawn from our other guides, not legal or financial advice.

  1. Confirm the rules. Rules and fees can change. Confirm them with the authority, or ask RFR, before you commit.
  2. Check the freehold status of the exact unit. Before you pay a deposit, confirm that the plot or building sits inside a designated freehold area.
  3. Check the developer, the plan and the plot. Look at the developer’s track record, the payment plan and the exact plot before you sign.
  4. Get the payment schedule in writing. For off-plan, ask for the SPA schedule, work out how much you pay before handover, and ask what happens if the handover date slips.
  5. Ask for the service charge. Running costs vary from building to building. Get the estimate in writing and include it in any yield sum.
  6. Look for evidence of rental demand. Ask for rents achieved in completed buildings nearby, not a projected yield, and check how much competing supply hands over in the same quarter.
  7. Budget the total cost. Add the 2% registration fee, AED 200 for plan issuance, AED 200 for the title deed, agency commission, furnishing and any developer charges.

( 09 ) FAQ

Common questions

Is Ras Al Khaimah a good place to buy property?

It can be, depending on your goal. Foreigners can buy freehold in the designated freehold areas, there is no income or capital gains tax on property in the UAE, and property worth AED 2M or more can qualify you for a UAE Golden Visa, subject to ICP rules. Against that, published 2026 data show price growth slowing, more than 34,000 new homes are expected by 2030, and nobody can promise what prices will do once Wynn Al Marjan Island opens.

Is Ras Al Khaimah property a good investment in 2026?

Nobody can promise a return. CBRE reported Al Marjan Island apartment values up 23.1% year on year in H1 2026, and ValuStrat put the overall Ras Al Khaimah index up 5.4% on the year in Q2 2026, but both sources show growth slowing during 2026. Past price changes are not a forecast. Check the developer, payment plan, handover date, service charge and evidence of rental demand for the specific unit.

Can foreigners buy property in Ras Al Khaimah?

Yes, in the designated freehold areas. Ras Al Khaimah Municipality lists them as Al Hamra Village, Mina Al Arab, Al Marjan Island, RAK Central, Julphar Towers, Al Maareedh (plots) and the upcoming Beach District. UAE and GCC nationals can buy anywhere in the Emirate.

Will Wynn Al Marjan Island make Ras Al Khaimah property prices rise?

Nobody can promise that. The resort is scheduled to open in September 2027. Published data show values rose year on year in 2026, fastest for Al Marjan Island apartments, but growth slowed during the year, and there is no data yet on prices after the resort opens.

How much does it cost to buy off-plan in Ras Al Khaimah?

Starting prices across the 9 Al Marjan Island launches in RFR’s current book run from AED 920,000 (Masa Residence) to AED 4,060,000 (W Hotel & Residences), with payment plans from 50/50 to 80/20. The floor, view and payment plan change the final figure. On top of the price, the buyer pays a 2% registration fee plus AED 200 for plan issuance and AED 200 for the title deed.

Does buying property in Ras Al Khaimah give you a Golden Visa?

It can. Property worth AED 2M or more can qualify you for a UAE Golden Visa, subject to the federal ICP rules at the time you apply. Official pages differ on whether the property route gives 5 or 10 years: ICP and RAKEZ describe 10 years, while the u.ae portal lists 5 years for real estate investments. RFR does not issue visas or give immigration advice.

What are the risks of buying property in Ras Al Khaimah?

Slower price growth than earlier in 2026, a large supply pipeline (more than 34,000 units expected between 2026 and 2030, per CBRE), handover dates that can slip, service charges that vary by building, and rental demand around the new resort that is not yet known. Confirm the freehold status of the exact unit and the current rules with the authority before you pay a deposit.

Which area of Ras Al Khaimah is best to buy in?

It depends on your goal. RFR’s buying guide matches resort-led off-plan to Al Marjan Island, yield to Al Hamra Village (or Downtown RAK) and space to Mina Al Arab. On RFR’s indicative ranges, based on RFR listings and public launch data, Al Hamra Village has the highest gross yield of the three coastal areas, at about 7–8.5%.
