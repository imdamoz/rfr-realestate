Golden Visa via Ras Al Khaimah property

The AED 2 million rule, what counts, and what the official pages disagree on.

BY THE RFR ADVISORY TEAM · PUBLISHED 15 NOVEMBER 2026

Official UAE sources set the property route to a Golden Visa at property worth at least AED 2 million in total, completed or off-plan, owned in your name. They do not currently agree on how long the visa lasts: ICP and RAKEZ describe 10 years, while the u.ae portal’s eligibility table lists 5 years for real estate investments.

Our homepage says “a Golden Visa from AED 2M”. That is a summary, not the rule. This guide sets out what the official pages say, as checked on 15 November 2026, so you can take the right questions to the authority. RFR helps people buy property in Ras Al Khaimah. We are not immigration lawyers, we do not issue visas and we cannot promise that any application will be approved.

General information only. This guide is not legal, financial, tax or immigration advice. Visa rules, thresholds and document lists change, sometimes without notice. Confirm the current requirements, and the length of the visa you would receive, with ICP or the relevant Ras Al Khaimah channel (such as RAKEZ) before you buy or apply. No purchase guarantees a visa.

( 01 ) THE THRESHOLD

How much property do you need?

ICP’s Golden Residency guide says the investor must own one or more properties in the UAE with a total value of at least AED 2,000,000, in full ownership in the investor’s name. RAKEZ’s Golden Visa page for property owners also sets a minimum value of AED 2 million and says multiple properties under the applicant’s name are acceptable.

In Ras Al Khaimah, RAKEZ asks for a Real Estate Valuation Certificate from the Real Estate Registration Department, addressed to Ras Al Khaimah ICP. The value that counts for the application is the one on the official documents, so do not assume a purchase price at or just above AED 2 million will be accepted as meeting the threshold. Check it before you commit.

( 02 ) COMPLETED OR OFF-PLAN

Does off-plan property count?

Both official pages allow off-plan property, with conditions:

  • ICP: one or more off-plan units with a total value of at least AED 2,000,000, bought from an approved local real estate company authorised by the competent local authority.
  • RAKEZ: the property, whether off-plan or completed, must be worth at least AED 2 million. For off-plan, the documents include a letter from the developer confirming the value and a sale and purchase agreement signed and stamped by the developer.

If you are buying off-plan on Al Marjan Island with a visa in mind, ask the developer early whether it can provide those documents. Not sure you can buy there as a foreign national? Our guide to whether foreigners can buy property in Ras Al Khaimah lists the designated freehold areas. See our Al Marjan Island guide and the current launches.

( 03 ) MORTGAGES

Can you buy with a mortgage?

ICP’s guide says the property can be financed through a loan if the loan is from an approved local bank. If you plan to borrow, ask the bank and the authority how a mortgaged property is assessed for your application before you sign, and get the answer in writing.

( 04 ) 5 OR 10 YEARS

Is it a 5-year or a 10-year visa?

The official pages we checked do not agree:

OFFICIAL SOURCEPAGE DATETERM STATED FOR REAL ESTATE INVESTORS
ICP, UAE Golden ResidencyLast updated 10 September 202610 years
RAKEZ, Green & Golden Visa ServicesNot dated; checked 15 November 202610 years
u.ae, Golden visa28 July 20265 years for real estate investments (10 years for public investments), with a minimum capital of AED 2 million

We are not in a position to say which is correct for your case. Before you buy for a visa, ask ICP, or the Ras Al Khaimah channel you will apply through, to confirm in writing the term you would receive, at the time you apply.

( 05 ) APPLYING IN RAK

Applying through a Ras Al Khaimah channel

The Golden Visa is a federal UAE residency, run by ICP, but you apply through a channel such as the relevant emirate service. RAKEZ lists a property Golden Visa service. Its document list includes:

  • A passport valid for at least six months
  • A photograph
  • Valid health insurance from the immigration-approved list
  • A Real Estate Valuation Certificate from the Real Estate Registration Department, addressed to Ras Al Khaimah ICP
  • For off-plan: a letter from the developer confirming the value, and the SPA signed and stamped by the developer

RAKEZ states that the applicant must be inside the UAE when submitting the visa request. If you are buying from abroad, plan a trip for the submission.

On health insurance, ICP says the investor and their family must hold comprehensive, valid health insurance throughout the residency. ICP’s page also lists benefits for holders, including residence without a sponsor and residence for family members; read the current list on the ICP page rather than relying on a summary.

( 06 ) STEP BY STEP

A sensible order of steps

  1. Confirm the rules first. Check the current threshold, the term and the document list with ICP or your RAK channel.
  2. Budget beyond AED 2 million. Add the 2% registration fee and other costs from our buying guide.
  3. Choose property that can be documented. Completed or off-plan, in your name, with the valuation or developer documents the channel asks for.
  4. Arrange health insurance. From the immigration-approved list, for you and any family you include.
  5. Be in the UAE to submit. RAKEZ requires the applicant to be inside the UAE when the request is submitted.
  6. Get the outcome in writing. Including the term of the residency granted.

( 08 ) FAQ

Common questions

How much property do I need to buy in Ras Al Khaimah for a Golden Visa?

Official sources set the minimum at property worth AED 2 million in total, owned in your name. In Ras Al Khaimah, RAKEZ asks for a valuation certificate from the Real Estate Registration Department, so confirm the documented value meets the threshold before you buy.

Can I get a Golden Visa with an off-plan property in Ras Al Khaimah?

Yes, according to ICP and RAKEZ, if the off-plan unit or units total at least AED 2 million. ICP requires the developer to be an approved local real estate company authorised by the competent local authority; RAKEZ asks for a developer letter confirming the value and a signed and stamped SPA.

Can I combine two or more properties to reach AED 2 million?

ICP refers to one or more properties with a total value of at least AED 2,000,000, and RAKEZ says multiple properties under the applicant’s name are acceptable.

Can I use a mortgage for a property Golden Visa?

ICP says financing through a loan is allowed if it is from an approved local bank. Confirm how a mortgaged property is assessed with the bank and the authority before you sign.

Is the property Golden Visa 5 years or 10 years?

Official sources differ. ICP and RAKEZ describe 10 years for real estate investors, while the u.ae portal’s table (dated 28 July 2026) lists 5 years for real estate investments. Ask ICP or your RAK channel to confirm the term in writing when you apply.

Do I need to be in the UAE to apply?

RAKEZ states that the applicant must be inside the UAE when submitting the visa request.

Does RFR arrange or guarantee Golden Visas?

No. RFR helps you find and buy property in Ras Al Khaimah. We do not issue visas, are not immigration lawyers and cannot guarantee approval.
