Al Hamra Village, Ras Al Khaimah

Golf, marina and the deepest rental market in RAK.

Al Hamra Village is Ras Al Khaimah’s original master community: an 18-hole championship golf course, a marina and yacht club, the Waldorf Astoria, a mall and a decade of tenants. It is one of the Emirate’s designated freehold areas, so foreign buyers can own property there.

( 01 ) WHAT IT IS

What is Al Hamra Village?

Al Hamra Village is the Emirate’s original master community: an 18-hole championship golf course, a marina and yacht club, the Waldorf Astoria, a mall and a decade of tenants. Falcon Island, its private villa enclave, sits within it.

Ras Al Khaimah Municipality lists Al Hamra Village among the Emirate’s designated freehold areas, alongside Mina Al Arab, Al Marjan Island, RAK Central, Julphar Towers, Al Maareedh (plots) and the upcoming Beach District. As our buying guide notes, UAE and GCC nationals can buy anywhere in the Emirate, and before you pay a deposit you should confirm that the exact plot or building sits inside a freehold area.

( 05 ) BUYING HERE

How buying works in Al Hamra Village

The rules and fees for Al Hamra Village are the same as for Ras Al Khaimah’s other freehold areas. The figures below are reused from our guide to buying property in Ras Al Khaimah, which cites Ras Al Khaimah Municipality’s published service information.

  • Registration fee: 2% of the property’s market value, paid by the buyer. The seller pays a further 2%.
  • Issuance fees: AED 200 for plan issuance and AED 200 for the title deed.
  • Mortgage registration: 0.1% of the loan, only where there is a mortgage.
  • Agency commission and developer charges: separate, and not set by the authorities.

The buying guide’s step-by-step section suggests matching your goal to the area, and lists yield in Downtown RAK or Al Hamra Village alongside space in Mina Al Arab and resort-led off-plan on Al Marjan Island. It also covers documents for a resale, the developer NOC and the transfer at RAK Municipality. The last step is to move in, or let RFR’s property management team find and manage tenants. If you are abroad, RFR runs video viewings for overseas buyers.

Property worth AED 2M or more can qualify you for a UAE Golden Visa, subject to the federal ICP rules at the time you apply. If the visa is part of the plan, read Golden Visa via RAK property first, because it shapes the budget and the choice of unit.

( 06 ) FAQ

Common questions

Can foreigners buy property in Al Hamra Village?

Yes. Al Hamra Village is one of Ras Al Khaimah’s designated freehold areas, so foreign buyers can own property there. RAK Municipality lists the freehold areas as Al Hamra Village, Mina Al Arab, Al Marjan Island, RAK Central, Julphar Towers, Al Maareedh (plots) and the upcoming Beach District.

How much does property cost in Al Hamra Village?

Apartments start from about AED 500K, and townhouses and villas sit at about AED 1.5–5M. These ranges are indicative, based on RFR listings and public launch data.

How much is rent in Al Hamra Village?

A one-bedroom rents for about AED 45–65K a year and a three-bedroom villa for about AED 110–160K a year. Figures are indicative; exact rents for any unit are available on request.

What rental yield can I expect in Al Hamra Village?

About 7–8.5% gross, as an indicative range. It is the closest established community to Al Marjan Island, so it’s where the resort staff arriving in 2027 will rent.

Who lives in Al Hamra Village and what is nearby?

Families, golfers, Al Hamra and Wynn workforce, and investors who want tenants in place from day one. Al Hamra Golf Club, Al Hamra Marina & Yacht Club, Al Hamra Mall, the Waldorf Astoria and Falcon Island are on the doorstep, and Dubai (DXB) is about 50 minutes away.

What are the government fees when you buy in Al Hamra Village?

The same as elsewhere in Ras Al Khaimah, as set out in our buying guide: the buyer pays a registration fee of 2% of the market value, plus AED 200 for plan issuance and AED 200 for the title deed. If you use a mortgage, its registration costs 0.1% of the loan. Agency commission and developer charges are separate.
