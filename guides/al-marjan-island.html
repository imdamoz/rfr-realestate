Al Marjan Island, Ras Al Khaimah

A 2026 guide to the islands, the homes and the published numbers.

BY THE RFR ADVISORY TEAM · PUBLISHED 15 NOVEMBER 2026

Al Marjan Island is a group of four man-made, coral-shaped islands off the Al Hamra coast of Ras Al Khaimah. It is one of the Emirate’s designated freehold areas, so foreign buyers can own property there, and it is where most of RAK’s new branded residences and the Wynn Al Marjan Island resort are being built.

This guide covers where the island sits, the kinds of homes on it, what has been reported about the Wynn resort, what published 2026 price data shows and how RFR works there. For prices, rents and what is nearby at a glance, see our Al Marjan Island area page.

( 01 ) WHERE IT IS

Where is Al Marjan Island?

Al Marjan Island lies just off the Al Hamra coast of Ras Al Khaimah. It is made up of four man-made, coral-shaped islands, and the whole group is master-planned. From the island, Al Hamra Mall and Al Hamra Marina are about 12 minutes by car, RAK Airport about 35 minutes and Dubai International (DXB) about 55 minutes.

Ras Al Khaimah Municipality lists Al Marjan Island among the Emirate’s designated freehold areas, alongside Al Hamra Village, Mina Al Arab, RAK Central, Julphar Towers, Al Maareedh (plots) and the upcoming Beach District. UAE and GCC nationals can buy anywhere in the Emirate. You can also explore the island on our map.

( 02 ) THE HOMES

What kinds of homes are on the island?

Most new supply is off-plan: branded residences with a hotel component, sold on payment plans. The launches in RFR’s current book include Address Residences (Emaar), Nobu Residences, JW Marriott Residences, Nikki Beach Residences (Aldar), W Hotel & Residences, Costa Mare (Ellington), Manta Bay, Sora Beach Residences and Masa Residence.

  • Off-plan launches: 9 on Al Marjan in our book, with developer entry prices from AED 0.92M to AED 4.06M.
  • Payment plans: from 50/50 to 80/20, with handovers in our book running from Q4 2026 to Q4 2028.
  • Rentals: furnished one-bedrooms in our book let for about AED 65,000–90,000 a year.

These are figures from RFR’s own listings and public launch data, not official market statistics. See the current off-plan launches, homes for sale and rent, or our September 2026 market note for our view.

( 03 ) THE WYNN RESORT

Wynn Al Marjan Island: what has been reported

Wynn Al Marjan Island is an integrated resort being built on Al Marjan Island. Press coverage describes it as a joint venture of Wynn Resorts, Marjan and RAK Hospitality Holding. According to Gulf News and Khaleej Times coverage of Wynn Resorts’ commentary, it is scheduled to open in September 2027, and the project budget was raised by about USD 600 million to around USD 5.7 billion (reported with the company’s August 2026 earnings).

CBRE’s H1 2026 review, published on 21 September 2026, still referred to a USD 5.1 billion resort expected to open in September 2027. The two cost figures come from different sources at different dates; the opening target is the same in both.

Opening dates and budgets for projects of this size can change. For what the resort may or may not mean for prices, read Wynn Al Marjan and RAK property prices.

( 06 ) FAQ

Common questions

Can foreigners buy property on Al Marjan Island?

Yes. Al Marjan Island is one of Ras Al Khaimah’s designated freehold areas, so foreign buyers can own property there. RAK Municipality lists the freehold areas as Al Hamra Village, Mina Al Arab, Al Marjan Island, RAK Central, Julphar Towers, Al Maareedh (plots) and the upcoming Beach District.

When does Wynn Al Marjan Island open?

It is scheduled to open in September 2027, according to Gulf News and Khaleej Times coverage of Wynn Resorts’ commentary, checked on 15 November 2026. Opening dates can change.

How much have Al Marjan Island prices changed in 2026?

CBRE reported Al Marjan Island apartment values up 23.1% year on year in H1 2026. ValuStrat reported Al Marjan apartments up 9.4% a year in Q2 2026, unchanged on the quarter. The two use different methods and periods, and past changes are not a forecast.

What does an apartment on Al Marjan Island cost?

Developer entry prices across the 9 Al Marjan launches in RFR’s book run from AED 0.92M to AED 4.06M. ValuStrat put the average Al Marjan apartment capital value at AED 928,000 per unit in Q2 2026. Budget a further 2% of market value for the buyer’s registration fee.

Can a property on Al Marjan Island get me a UAE Golden Visa?

It may, if the property (or properties) you own are worth at least AED 2 million and meet the other official conditions. Official sources currently differ on whether the property route gives 5 or 10 years. See our Golden Visa guide and confirm with ICP or your RAK application channel.

Is everything on Al Marjan Island off-plan?

No. Most new supply is off-plan, but there are completed buildings on the island with homes to rent and resell. RFR’s listings include furnished rentals there.
