Buying property in Ras Al Khaimah

A 2026 guide for UAE residents and overseas buyers.

BY THE RFR ADVISORY TEAM · UPDATED 15 NOVEMBER 2026

Foreigners can buy freehold property in Ras Al Khaimah’s designated freehold areas, including Al Marjan Island, Al Hamra Village and Mina Al Arab. On top of the price, budget 2% of the market value for registration, plus small issuance fees.

This guide covers who can buy, what it costs, the paperwork and each step from shortlist to handover. It is written for people buying to live, to let or for a Golden Visa, whether you are in the UAE or abroad. If a term is new to you, such as SPA, NOC or title deed, our Ras Al Khaimah property glossary explains it in plain English. Still deciding whether to buy in the Emirate at all? Is Ras Al Khaimah a good place to buy property? weighs the case for buying against the risks.

( 01 ) WHO CAN BUY

Who can buy property in Ras Al Khaimah?

UAE and GCC nationals can buy property anywhere in Ras Al Khaimah. Other foreign nationals can buy freehold in the Emirate’s designated freehold areas, which Ras Al Khaimah Municipality lists as:

  • Al Hamra Village
  • Mina Al Arab
  • Al Marjan Island
  • RAK Central
  • Julphar Towers
  • Al Maareedh (plots)
  • The upcoming Beach District

Before you pay a deposit, confirm that the exact plot or building sits inside a freehold area. Ask the developer or RFR to check it for the specific unit; in Downtown RAK, for example, freehold is limited to designated projects such as Julphar Towers and RAK Central.

For the ownership question on its own, including who can buy where and what freehold means, see can foreigners buy property in Ras Al Khaimah?

( 02 ) FEES

What does it cost to buy?

The main government cost is the registration fee: 2% of the property’s market value, paid by the buyer. The seller pays a further 2%. Two small issuance fees and, if you borrow, a mortgage registration fee are added on top.

ITEMAMOUNTNOTES

As a worked example, on an AED 1,000,000 apartment bought without a mortgage the buyer’s government fees come to AED 20,400: AED 20,000 for registration and AED 400 for the plan and title deed.

Source: Ras Al Khaimah Municipality, Lands & Properties Sector, published service information.

( 03 ) DOCUMENTS

Documents for a resale purchase

For a ready (resale) property, expect to provide or see:

  • Emirates ID or passport
  • A no-objection certificate (NOC) from the developer, for freehold areas
  • The seller’s original title deed
  • A power of attorney, if an agent acts for either party

For an off-plan unit you sign the developer’s sale and purchase agreement (SPA) and pay against its schedule.

( 04 ) OFF-PLAN

Buying off-plan on Al Marjan Island

Most new supply in Ras Al Khaimah is off-plan on Al Marjan Island. RFR’s current book has 9 launches there, with entry prices from AED 920K to AED 4.06M. Payment plans run from 50/50 to 80/20, so part of the price is paid during construction and the balance typically on handover.

Wynn Al Marjan Island is scheduled to open in 2027, and most launches are priced against that date. Pick the plan and the handover date before the brand. ( 05 ) TAX AND VISAS

Tax and the Golden Visa

There is no income tax or capital gains tax on property in the UAE. Property worth AED 2M or more can qualify you for a UAE Golden Visa, subject to the federal ICP rules at the time you apply. If the visa is the goal, tell us at the start, because it shapes the budget and the choice of unit. Official pages currently differ on whether the property route gives 5 or 10 years; our Golden Visa via RAK property guide sets out what ICP, u.ae and RAKEZ each say.

( 06 ) WHERE TO BUY

The four areas RFR covers

Al Marjan Island: the man-made islands where most of RAK’s branded residences are being built and where Wynn opens in 2027. Studios from about AED 920K, one- and two-bedroom branded residences about AED 1.7–3.5M, and about 6–8% gross yield.

Mina Al Arab: a low-rise, family-led community built around lagoons and mangroves. Apartments from about AED 800K, villas about AED 2.5–6M, and about 5.5–7% gross yield.

Al Hamra Village: the Emirate’s original master community, with golf, a marina and the deepest rental market in RAK. Apartments from about AED 500K, townhouses and villas about AED 1.5–5M, and about 7–8.5% gross yield.

Downtown RAK: the working city around the corniche, hospitals and offices. Apartments from about AED 400K and the highest yields of the four, at about 7–9% gross; freehold is limited to designated projects.

All ranges are indicative, based on RFR listings and public launch data.

( 07 ) STEP BY STEP

How to buy, step by step

  1. Set your budget and goal. Decide whether the property is to live in, to rent out or for a Golden Visa, and include the 2% registration fee in the budget.
  2. Shortlist an area. Match the goal to the area: yield in Downtown RAK or Al Hamra Village, space in Mina Al Arab, resort-led off-plan on Al Marjan Island.
  3. View the property. In person, or on video if you are abroad; RFR runs video viewings for overseas buyers.
  4. Reserve and sign. Agree the price and sign a memorandum of understanding (MOU) for a resale, or the developer’s SPA for off-plan.
  5. Get the developer NOC. In freehold areas the developer issues a no-objection certificate before the transfer.
  6. Transfer and register. Complete the transfer at RAK Municipality, pay the registration fee and receive the title deed.
  7. Take handover, or let it out. Move in, or let RFR’s property management team find and manage tenants.
( 08 ) FAQ

Common questions

Can foreigners buy property in Ras Al Khaimah?

Yes, in the designated freehold areas. Ras Al Khaimah Municipality lists them as Al Hamra Village, Mina Al Arab, Al Marjan Island, RAK Central, Julphar Towers, Al Maareedh (plots) and the upcoming Beach District. UAE and GCC nationals can buy anywhere in the Emirate.

How much are the government fees when you buy in Ras Al Khaimah?

The buyer pays a registration fee of 2% of the market value, plus AED 200 for plan issuance and AED 200 for the title deed. If you use a mortgage, its registration costs 0.1% of the loan. Agency commission and developer charges are separate.

Do I need to be in the UAE to buy property in Ras Al Khaimah?

Not necessarily. RFR runs video viewings for overseas buyers, and a power of attorney can be used if someone acts for you at the transfer.

Can buying property in Ras Al Khaimah get me a Golden Visa?

It can. Property worth AED 2M or more can qualify you for a UAE Golden Visa, subject to the federal ICP rules at the time you apply.

Is there tax on property in the UAE?

There is no income tax or capital gains tax on property in the UAE. The registration fees above still apply when you buy.

What payment plans do off-plan projects on Al Marjan Island offer?

Plans run from 50/50 to 80/20 across the 9 Al Marjan launches in RFR’s current book, with entry prices from AED 920K to AED 4.06M.
