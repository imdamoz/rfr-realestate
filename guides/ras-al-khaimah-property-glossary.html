Ras Al Khaimah property glossary

Plain-English meanings of the words we use on this site.

BY THE RFR ADVISORY TEAM · PUBLISHED 16 NOVEMBER 2026 · UPDATED 16 NOVEMBER 2026

This glossary explains, in plain English, the 29 property terms used across RFR’s pages, from off-plan, payment plan and handover to freehold, title deed and gross yield. Each definition is general, matches how we use the word on this site and links to the page where we discuss it. It is not legal or financial advice; fees and rules change, so confirm details with the relevant authority or RFR.

Terms are grouped by topic, with an A to Z list below. Each definition matches how the word is used on rfrrealestate.com, and adds no fees, rules or thresholds that our other pages do not already state.

General information only. These are plain-English explanations, not legal, financial, tax or immigration advice. Rules and fees change; confirm anything you rely on with the relevant authority, the developer or RFR before you act.

( 01 ) BUYING

Buying and owning

Agency commission

The fee you pay an estate agent for a sale or a letting. It is agreed with the agent and is not set by the authorities. RFR’s published fees are 2% for sales and 5% for leasing, plus VAT, agreed in writing before we start.

Where we discuss it: Services · Buying guide

Freehold

Full ownership of a property in your own name. In Ras Al Khaimah, foreign buyers can buy freehold in the Emirate’s designated freehold areas, which Ras Al Khaimah Municipality lists as Al Hamra Village, Mina Al Arab, Al Marjan Island, RAK Central, Julphar Towers, Al Maareedh (plots) and the upcoming Beach District. UAE and GCC nationals can buy anywhere in the Emirate. Before you pay a deposit, confirm that the exact plot or building sits inside a freehold area.

Where we discuss it: Buying guide · Area comparison

Golden Visa

A long-term UAE residence visa. One route is through property: official sources set it at property worth at least AED 2 million in total, owned in your name, subject to the federal ICP rules at the time you apply. Official pages currently differ on whether the property route gives 5 or 10 years. RFR does not issue visas or give immigration advice.

Where we discuss it: Golden Visa guide

MOU (memorandum of understanding)

The agreement a buyer and seller sign once they have agreed the price on a resale property, before the transfer.

Where we discuss it: Buying guide

NOC (no-objection certificate)

A certificate from the developer saying it has no objection to the sale. In Ras Al Khaimah’s freehold areas the developer issues it before the transfer, and it is one of the documents for a resale purchase.

Where we discuss it: Buying guide

Power of attorney

A legal document that lets someone act for you. It is on the resale document list if an agent acts for either party, and it can be used if someone acts for you at the transfer, for example when you are buying from abroad.

Where we discuss it: Buying guide

Registration fee

The government fee paid when a purchase is registered at RAK Municipality. As set out in our buying guide, the buyer pays 2% of the market value and the seller a further 2%; plan issuance and title deed issuance cost AED 200 each, and mortgage registration is 0.1% of the loan where there is a mortgage.

Where we discuss it: Buying guide

Resale (ready property)

A completed property bought from its current owner rather than from a developer. On a resale you sign an MOU, and the documents include the seller’s original title deed and a developer NOC.

Where we discuss it: Buying guide · Listings

Title deed

The official document that shows who owns a property. You receive it once the transfer is registered at RAK Municipality, and on a resale the seller’s original title deed is one of the documents you will see.

Where we discuss it: Buying guide

( 02 ) OFF-PLAN

Off-plan

Branded residence

A home in a building that carries a hotel or lifestyle brand, usually with a hotel component and hotel-style services. Nearly every off-plan launch on Al Marjan Island in our book is a branded residence with a hotel component, such as Address Residences, Nobu Residences and W Hotel & Residences.

Where we discuss it: Market note · Al Marjan Island guide

Handover

When the developer completes an off-plan unit and hands it to the buyer. On the plans in our book, part of the price is paid during construction and the balance typically on handover. Each launch page shows the developer’s published handover date; dates can change, so ask what the SPA says if a date slips.

Where we discuss it: Payment plans guide · Launches compared

Off-plan

Signing for a home before it’s finished. Building work may have started, but you can’t walk through your unit, so you decide from renders, floor plans, virtual tours and a visit to the site. You pay in instalments as it’s built.

Where we discuss it: Off-plan launches · Payment plans guide

Payment plan

How a developer splits the price of an off-plan unit into stages. Plans are written as two numbers, such as 60/40: part of the price is paid during construction and the balance typically on handover. Across the launches in our book, plans run from 50/50 to 80/20. The exact instalments and due dates are set in the developer’s SPA, so ask for the schedule in writing.

Where we discuss it: Payment plans guide · Launches compared

SPA (sale and purchase agreement)

The contract you sign with the developer when you buy off-plan. You pay against its schedule and it is what binds you, so check what it says about the payment schedule and about a late handover.

Where we discuss it: Buying guide · Wynn guide

Starting price

The lowest price published for a project, shown as “from” on our launch pages. It is not the price of every unit: the floor, view and payment plan change the figure. Launch prices usually undercut finished homes, and early units cost least.

Where we discuss it: Off-plan launches · Launches compared

Unit mix

The range of home types in a project, for example “Studios–3 bed” or “1–4 bed, penthouses”. Each launch page lists its unit mix.

Where we discuss it: Launches compared

( 03 ) RENTING

Renting and returns

Gross yield

A year’s rent as a percentage of the property’s price, before running costs such as the service charge. Our area pages give indicative ranges, such as about 7–8.5% gross in Al Hamra Village and about 6–8% on Al Marjan Island. They are not a forecast; ask for the figures on a specific unit.

Where we discuss it: Areas · Area comparison

Holiday let

A furnished home let for short stays rather than to a long-term tenant. Our Al Marjan Island area page lists holiday-let owners among the people who own there.

Where we discuss it: Al Marjan Island

Net yield

The return after running costs. The calculator on our homepage shows net yield on cash invested: the yearly net cash flow (expected rent, less service charge and mortgage payments) as a share of the cash you put in to buy. It excludes maintenance, vacancy and furnishing.

Where we discuss it: Homepage calculator · Downtown RAK

Property management

Looking after a let property for its owner: finding and screening tenants and managing the unit, which suits owners who live abroad. RFR’s property management fee starts from 5% of annual rent, plus VAT.

Where we discuss it: Services

Rental pool

An arrangement in which a building’s operator lets and manages owners’ units on their behalf. JW Marriott Residences on Al Marjan Island includes a rental pool for owners who live abroad.

Where we discuss it: JW Marriott Residences

Rental yield

The rent a property earns as a percentage of its price. Our pages quote gross yield; see also net yield.

Where we discuss it: Areas

Service charge

The yearly charge owners pay towards running and maintaining a building and its shared areas. Running costs vary from building to building, so ask the developer for the estimated service charge in writing and include it in any yield sum.

Where we discuss it: Wynn guide · Homepage calculator

( 04 ) PROPERTY TYPES

Types of property

Duplex

An apartment laid out over two floors. RFR lists furnished two-bedroom duplexes for rent on Al Marjan Island and in Downtown RAK, and Nobu Residences includes duplexes in its unit mix.

Where we discuss it: Duplex on Al Marjan Island · Duplex in Downtown RAK

Master-planned community

A large development designed to one overall plan, with the homes, amenities and roads laid out together. Al Hamra Village is the Emirate’s original master community, and Al Marjan Island is fully master-planned.

Where we discuss it: Al Hamra Village · Al Marjan Island

Penthouse

An apartment on the top floor or floors of a building. Address Residences and Sora Beach Residences list penthouses in their unit mix.

Where we discuss it: Launches compared

Plot

A piece of land sold for building on, rather than a finished home. RFR lists a residential plot in Mina Al Arab, and RAK Municipality’s list of freehold areas includes Al Maareedh for plots.

Where we discuss it: Plot in Mina Al Arab · Buying guide

Studio

An apartment with one main room for living and sleeping, plus a bathroom and kitchen. On Al Marjan Island studios start from about AED 920K (indicative), and several launches include them.

Where we discuss it: Al Marjan Island · Studio at Manta Bay

Townhouse

A house that shares one or more walls with its neighbours, usually over two or more floors. Townhouses are among the homes in Al Hamra Village and Mina Al Arab.

Where we discuss it: Al Hamra Village · Mina Al Arab
