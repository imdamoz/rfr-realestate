( 02 ) IN NUMBERS

What the table shows

  • 9 launches, 9 developers, one area. Every launch in our book is on Al Marjan Island, and no developer has more than one.
  • Starting prices run from AED 920,000 (Masa Residence) to AED 4,060,000 (W Hotel & Residences). The middle launch by price starts at AED 2,100,000 (Costa Mare and Nobu Residences share that figure). Three start below AED 2,000,000: Masa Residence, Manta Bay and Nikki Beach Residences.
  • Payment plans run from 50/50 to 80/20. 60/40: 5 (Nobu Residences, JW Marriott Residences, Nikki Beach Residences, Sora Beach Residences and Masa Residence); 50/50: 2 (W Hotel & Residences and Manta Bay); 70/30: 1 (Costa Mare); 80/20: 1 (Address Residences).
  • Handovers as listed run from Q4 2026 to Q4 2028: 3 in 2026, 2 in 2027 and 4 in 2028. ( 03 ) BEFORE YOU RESERVE

What to check before you reserve

  • The developer, the plan and the plot. Check the developer’s track record, the payment plan and the exact plot before you sign.
  • The SPA. For off-plan you sign the developer’s SPA and pay against its schedule. Ask for the schedule in writing, and ask what happens if the handover date slips.
  • Availability and the final price. Starting prices change with floor, view and payment plan. Ask RFR what is still available.
  • Government fees. Budget the buyer’s registration fee of 2% of market value, plus AED 200 for plan issuance and AED 200 for the title deed, as set out in our buying guide.

Terms such as SPA, handover and unit mix are explained in our property glossary. For the island itself, see the Al Marjan Island area page, our Al Marjan Island guide and what Wynn means (and doesn’t) for prices. Comparing areas? See Al Marjan Island vs Al Hamra Village vs Mina Al Arab.

( 04 ) FAQ

Common questions

How many off-plan launches does RFR list in Ras Al Khaimah?

Nine, all on Al Marjan Island, each from a different developer: Address Residences (Emaar), Nobu Residences (H&H Development), JW Marriott Residences (WOW Resorts), Nikki Beach Residences (Aldar), W Hotel & Residences (Dalands), Costa Mare (Ellington), Manta Bay (Major Developers), Sora Beach Residences (AARK) and Masa Residence (Durar Group). Figures as listed on rfrrealestate.com on 16 November 2026.

Which off-plan launch in RFR’s book has the lowest starting price?

Masa Residence by Durar Group, from AED 920,000, followed by Manta Bay from AED 1,200,000. These are starting prices; the floor, view and payment plan change the figure.

Which launch has the highest starting price?

W Hotel & Residences by Dalands, from AED 4,060,000, with a 50/50 payment plan and handover listed for Dec 2027.

Which launches hand over first?

Manta Bay, Sora Beach Residences and Masa Residence, all listed for Q4 2026. The latest listed handover is Nikki Beach Residences, in Q4 2028. Handover dates are the developers’ published dates and can change.

What is the most common payment plan?

60/40, used by five of the nine launches: Nobu Residences, JW Marriott Residences, Nikki Beach Residences, Sora Beach Residences and Masa Residence. The others are 50/50 (W Hotel & Residences and Manta Bay), 70/30 (Costa Mare) and 80/20 (Address Residences).

Which launches include studios?

Costa Mare, Manta Bay, Masa Residence and Nikki Beach Residences list studios in their unit mix. Address Residences, JW Marriott Residences and W Hotel & Residences go up to four bedrooms.
