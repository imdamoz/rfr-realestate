Off-plan payment plans in Ras Al Khaimah

What off-plan means, and the plans in our current book, exactly as published.

BY THE RFR ADVISORY TEAM · PUBLISHED 15 NOVEMBER 2026

Most new supply in Ras Al Khaimah is off-plan on Al Marjan Island. RFR’s current book has 9 launches there, with entry prices from AED 920K to AED 4.06M. Payment plans run from 50/50 to 80/20, so part of the price is paid during construction and the balance typically on handover.

This explainer sets out what off-plan means, lists the payment plan, starting price and handover date of each launch in our book exactly as published on its launch page, and points to what to check before you sign. It adds no figures that are not already on our off-plan page, the nine launch pages and our buying guide.

Prices, payment plans and handover dates are set by each developer and can change; the developer’s sale and purchase agreement (SPA) is what binds you. Confirm anything you rely on with the developer, the relevant authority or RFR before you act. Nothing here is a promise of returns.

( 01 ) WHAT OFF-PLAN MEANS

You’re buying something that isn’t built yet

Off-plan means signing for a home before it’s finished. Building work may have started, but you can’t walk through your unit, so you decide from renders, floor plans, virtual tours and a visit to the site.

That’s why it helps to have someone check the developer’s track record, the payment plan and the exact plot before you sign.

( 02 ) HOW THE PLANS WORK

Paying in stages

You pay in instalments as it’s built. Across the launches in our book, plans run from 50/50 to 80/20, so part of the price is paid during construction and the balance typically on handover.

For an off-plan unit you sign the developer’s sale and purchase agreement (SPA) and pay against its schedule. The exact instalments and when each falls due are set by that developer’s SPA for that unit, so ask for the schedule in writing before you reserve. We list each plan below exactly as the developer’s launch is published; we do not publish month-by-month schedules.

Across the 9 launches as published:

  • 50 / 50: Manta Bay, W Hotel & Residences
  • 60 / 40: JW Marriott Residences, Masa Residence, Nikki Beach Residences, Nobu Residences, Sora Beach Residences
  • 70 / 30: Costa Mare
  • 80 / 20: Address Residences

( 04 ) WHY PEOPLE BUY OFF-PLAN

The case for off-plan, and its limits

Our off-plan page gives three reasons buyers look at launches:

  • Lower prices at launch. Launch prices usually undercut finished homes, and early units cost least.
  • Near the new resorts. Most launches are on the waterfront, by the resorts.
  • Pay in stages. You pay in instalments as it’s built.

None of these is a guarantee. A launch price is a starting price, and the floor, view and payment plan change the final figure. Wynn Al Marjan Island is scheduled to open in 2027, and most launches are priced against that date. Pick the plan and the handover date before the brand. For what has been reported about the resort, and what published data does and does not show, read Wynn Al Marjan and RAK property prices and our Al Marjan Island guide.

( 05 ) BEFORE YOU SIGN

What to check before you reserve

From our off-plan page and buying guide:

  • The developer, the plan and the plot. Check the developer’s track record, the payment plan and the exact plot before you sign.
  • Freehold. Confirm that the exact plot or building sits inside a freehold area. Al Marjan Island is one of the designated freehold areas listed by Ras Al Khaimah Municipality.
  • The SPA. For off-plan you sign the developer’s SPA and pay against its schedule.
  • Availability and the final price. Starting prices change with floor, view and payment plan. Ask RFR what is still available before you reserve.
  • Government fees. Budget the buyer’s registration fee of 2% of market value, plus AED 200 for plan issuance and AED 200 for the title deed. Agency commission and developer charges are separate.

Our Wynn guide also lists questions to ask on any unit sold on the Wynn story, including what happens under the SPA if the handover date slips. The full process, from shortlist to handover, is in our guide to buying property in Ras Al Khaimah.

Property worth AED 2M or more can qualify you for a UAE Golden Visa, subject to the federal ICP rules at the time you apply. If the visa is the goal, tell us at the start, because it shapes the budget and the choice of unit. Read Golden Visa via RAK property for what the official sources say about off-plan units.
