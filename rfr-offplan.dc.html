WHAT "OFF-PLAN" MEANS

You’re buying something that isn’t built yet.

Off-plan means signing for a home before it’s finished. Building work may have started, but you can’t walk through your unit, so you decide from renders, floor plans, virtual tours and a visit to the site.

That’s why it helps to have someone check the developer’s track record, the payment plan and the exact plot before you sign.

/ 01
Lower prices at launch

Launch prices usually undercut finished homes, and early units cost least.

/ 02
Near the new resorts

Most launches are on the waterfront, by the resorts.

/ 03
Pay in stages

You pay in instalments as it’s built.
